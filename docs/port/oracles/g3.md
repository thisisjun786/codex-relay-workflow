## Shared helper added: `pyoracle.AnswerInterned`

`internal/testsupport/pyoracle/interned.go`, with its tests in `interned_test.go`, is a new file.
`pyoracle.go` is unchanged. `AnswerInterned` has `Answer`'s signature. When an answer is at least
32 KiB and its encoding is under half its size, the answer is recorded encoded (`Intern`) and
returned decoded (`Expand`). The encoding cuts the answer after every `}` and newline, stores each
distinct piece once, and writes the answer as runs of consecutive pieces. It is lossless whatever
the answer's format, and `Substitute` placeholders work on it as on a plain answer. It exists
because gzip only folds repeats within 32 KiB, and whole-table dumps taken after every step repeat
the same rows far apart. Routing and sync use it: routing's recordings went from 5.1M on disk
(2.7 MB compressed) to 3.8M (0.5 MB). Faults and adapter measured it and did not need it.

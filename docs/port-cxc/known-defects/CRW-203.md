# CRW-203 — hook parity harness: what firing the declared hooks showed

Source: the CRW-203 issue (Linear) and the coordinator decision of 2026-10-10 on it. The oracle files at v0.2.40 (commit 3c1459ac) are plugins/codexclaw/.codex-plugin/plugin.json (the 32 registrations, contract K1), scripts/hook-observation.mjs (the invocation record) and components/*/dist/cli.js (the hook entry points the latency cell times). The Go files are internal/dev/laneparity (the harness), internal/contracttest/cxc_hookfire.go (the firing through a declared command), internal/harness/observation.go (the invocation record) and cmd/crw/component_hooks.go and internal/harness/legs.go (the leg tables). The harness is described in docs/port-cxc/parity-harness.md.

- The plugin that ships (plugins/crw) declares one of the 34 legs, the completion Stop (.codex-plugin/plugin.json hooks, wiring/hooks/stop-recording-completion.json); the other 33 are handled by `crw hook` and exercised by the corpus replay, but no hook JSON registers them, so the host never starts them (the GitHub post guard is such a row, CRW-783.md). `crw-dev parity registration --plugin plugins/crw` reports each missing leg; `parity all` fires a root generated from K1 in its place. Declaring them is the activation PR's work (CRW-392); port: pending.
- Fired through the command a root declares (/bin/sh -c, PLUGIN_ROOT, an isolated CODEX_HOME), the 213 claimed hook fixtures and the 3 probes of CRW's own registrations (the GitHub post guard and the completion Stop) equal their expectations: the declared path and the direct `crw hook <event> --leg <leg>` path of TestDomain/cxc agree. No defect found there; port: kept.
- The hook invocation record (`<CODEX_HOME>/crw/hook-observations/<sha(session)>/<sha(agent)>/<sha(slot)>.json`) is written for the pabcd-state legs and one subagent-config row only, as in the oracle, but it names crw's own manifest as the entry point (.codex-plugin/plugin.json, there is no components/<name>/dist/cli.js) and carries the crw manifest version, so the record file is named differently. Two fixtures that observe the store (hook__session-start-bootstrapping-pabcd-state__observes_hook_invocation_record, hook__session-start-detecting-managed-worktree__unmanaged_and_root_are_silent) are claimed intentionally changed in contract/notes/cxc/CRW-203.json; the crw version (0.4.0+ and 12 hex digits of the payload digest, which every payload change moves) is normalised to <VERSION> by the new text rule crw-plugin-version of normalisation.json so the claim does not move with it; port: kept.
- Left pending by this issue, with the reason each stays: hook__stop-waking-on-background-completion__running_record_reconciled_from_exit_file prints the age of a job from the wall clock (405717m15s where the frozen clock of the recording gives 10m00s) and hook__session-start-injecting-recall-context__startup_without_index_advertises_recall prints "last successful extraction 281d ago" from the wall clock where the recording has none, so each needs a clock seam in its own component (bg-wake, recall); hook__pre-tool-use-guarding-goal-complete__bound_plan_with_open_work_denied equals its recording as it stands and is claimed by no issue, so whoever owns the goal-complete guard claims it identical; the 12 stop-checking-pabcd-continuation and 2 subagent-stop-observing-review fixtures stay with CRW-192 and CRW-564, the 13 spawn-attach and 5 memory-write fixtures with their issues; port: pending.
- The latency cell compares p95 of the Go command with p95 of the Node original and with half the declared timeout. On the shared host a p95 over 30 runs holds outliers of 100 ms to 2 s on both sides when the load average is above 20, so the cell alternates the two sides and measures a leg that fails again (three measurements in all) before reporting it; a leg that is really slower fails every attempt. Measured with the harness at the final head, in the evidence directory named in the handoff; port: kept.

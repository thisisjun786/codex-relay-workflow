# CRW-1092 — a completion is stamped after its text is written, and hooks claim it under one lock

Source: CXC v0.2.40 (commit 3c1459ac) `plugins/codexclaw/components/bg-wake/src/hook.ts:63-70` and `:99-119` (Stop, prompt and SessionStart), `registry.ts:217-250` (markDelivered, adoptOrphans), `cli.ts:62-77` (the hook's stdout). The Go files are `internal/relay/job/registry.go` (deliver, markDelivered, AdoptOrphans and adoptOrphans), `internal/relay/job/hook.go` (completion, runHook), `internal/relay/job/envelope.go` (writeHookOutput returns its error), `internal/relay/job/verbs.go` (cliDrain) and `internal/relay/job/lock.go` (the store lock of CRW-1155). The tests are in `internal/relay/job/hook_claim_test.go`; the red run against the code before the change is `/state/crw/crw-lane-bg-job/evidence/red-1092/red.log`. The at-most-once loss that `devlog/_plan/260909_bg_wake_component/000_plan.md:91-105` accepts against an endless Stop is narrowed, not replaced: there is no host acknowledgement and no replay.

- Supersedes known-defects.md, section "Found by the CRW-490 bg hooks port", item beginning "Stop, prompt delivery and drain stamp records before handing their output to the caller": now port: fixed for Stop and UserPromptSubmit (RunHook writes the envelope from inside the delivery and the completions are stamped only after the write returned without error; a write that fails, `/dev/full` or a closed pipe, and a hook process that dies before or after the write leave them pending for the next wake. Pinned by `TestBgHookStdoutThatFailsLeavesTheCompletionPending` and `TestBgHookCrashOrFullStdoutLeavesTheCompletionPending`, whose crash-after case shows the cost: a hook killed after its write and before its stamp hands the completion out again at the next wake). Drain keeps the stamp before the write: its text is the relay's answer, which the dispatcher writes after `RunParsedCLI` returns, so a lost drain answer still loses the wake; the result stays in `list` and `get`; port: kept for drain.
- Supersedes known-defects.md, section "Found by the CRW-490 bg hooks port", item beginning "Selection, delivery stamping and adoption are separate unlocked operations": now port: fixed (a Stop, a prompt and a drain select, emit and stamp while they hold the store's `flock` on the bg directory, and SessionStart adopts under it; every stamp and adoption re-reads the record and writes only its own field over the record as it is on disk, so a second hook sees the first one's stamp and an adoption never puts back an undelivered snapshot. The claim of a batch is the held lock: the batch is never handed out by a second hook while the first writes it, so no claim field is written and the records keep the oracle's thirteen keys. Pinned by `TestBgHookConcurrentHooksHandOutACompletionOnce`, which starts the second hook, Stop, prompt, drain or SessionStart, after the first one has read the store). A stamp write that fails still leaves the described completion to wake again; port: kept (at least once over at most once for a failed stamp).
- A store that another writer holds for longer than five seconds (`lockWait`) makes the hook emit nothing and leaves its completions pending; a store that does not exist is not created by a hook. Recorded deviation; port: fixed.
- SessionStart still adopts every undelivered completion of another session without asking whether that session is alive, and adopts while the wake is off: an owner lease without evidence of the session's life would stop adoption after a restart or a compaction, and the off-window meaning is a contract of its own (the issue's scope). Known-defects.md, section "Found by the CRW-490 bg hooks port", item beginning "SessionStart adopts every undelivered terminal record" stays port: kept.
- A Stop, a prompt or a drain that is waiting for the store lock while `off` holds it asks again whether the wake is suppressed once it has the lock: the switch is read under the same lock `off` writes it under, so a completion is not handed out and stamped after `job off` has returned. Drain keeps its exception (it collects with the wake off). Pinned by `TestAHookThatWaitedForTheLockHonoursAnOffThatFinishedFirst`; the environment switch is read again there too. Port: fixed (recorded deviation).

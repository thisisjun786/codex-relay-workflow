# CRW-1288 — the release of a lane is a compare-and-swap at the relay's release commit

Source: CRW's own design (0.5.0-alpha, the split of issue F09, CRW-1202), not the CXC oracle: CXC v0.2.40 (commit 3c1459ac) has no flow release and no plan slice to compare. The Go side is `internal/flow/ops/{seams,lane_design}.go` (`ReleaseRequest.SliceDigest`, `ReleaseResult.SliceDigest`, `crw.release`), `internal/flow/cli/schedrelay.go` (`SchedRelay.Release`, `holdBack`) and `internal/relay/dagsched/release.go` (`ReleaseRequest.ExpectedSliceDigest`, `expectSlice`, `judgeNodeRelease`, `rejudge`); the rule is written in `docs/flow/script.md`, `docs/flow/journal.md` and `docs/relay/dag-scheduler.md`, "Releasing a node". The tests are `TestSolReleaseMustCompareAtRelayCommit`, `TestReleaseJournalsTheCommittedSlice` and `TestReleaseRefusedForAPlanTheFlowDoesNotReadIsNotAskedAgain` (`internal/flow/ops/release_cas_test.go`), `TestReleaseComparesThePlanSliceAtTheRelayCommit` (`internal/relay/dagsched/release_cas_test.go`) and `TestSchedRelayReleaseComparesTheExpectedSlice` and `TestSchedRelayReplayUnderAnotherSliceIsNotReleased` (`internal/flow/cli/schedrelay_test.go`); the replay is pinned by `TestReleaseReplayComparesTheExpectedSlice` (`internal/relay/dagsched/release_cas_test.go`).

- Supersedes CRW-1202.md, item beginning "Named limit of that release check, carried to CRW-1288": now port: fixed (the release carries the node's slice of the plan the flow compared, `expected_slice_digest` of `dag-release-request/1`; the relay compares it with the node's slice of the plan's head when it judges the release and again first in the transaction that reserves the slot and records the release, on both surfaces; a slice that is another, or a node the head no longer holds, releases nothing and is refused `plan_revision_conflict`, which the run's relay answers as `released: false`, `plan_changed`, so the lane goes back through the plan read, the signal, the re-plan and the audit that CRW-1202 built). CRW-1202.md's item now states the rule in the present tense.
- The `slice_digest` of a released `crw.release` row is the slice of the manifest the relay committed (`ReleaseResult.SliceDigest`, read back from the frozen manifest's `node_slice_digest`), not the slice the flow read. A replay is a compare-and-swap too (`expectReplayed`): the relay answers the replay of a release, on both surfaces and for the replay of a concurrent release, only to a request whose expected slice is the one the release was committed under (the frozen manifest's `node_slice_digest`), whatever the plan's head is now; a run that stopped after the relay's commit and before its `crw.release` row, and asks again under the slice of a plan that changed and was re-planned since, gets `plan_revision_conflict` (`plan_changed`), not a release under the new slice, and nothing is written. An unreleased row keeps the slice the flow asked for. port: fixed.
- The issue body does not say what the lane does when the relay refuses a slice the flow cannot see: the run reads its plan at the revision it pinned (`SchedRelay.Milestone`) while the relay releases from the plan's head, so a plan revision put on the relay during a run changes the head's slice and never the flow's plan. Answered as `plan_changed`, the lane would read the plan again, find no trigger and ask again without end. When the plan the flow reads again after such a refusal is still the pinned one, `crw.release` answers `relay_plan_changed`. The signal, re-plan and audit loop the body names is not closed for such a change: the run's plan is its pinned revision by design (CRW-1277 c: `SchedRelay.Milestone`, `issueOf` and the handoff the run pinned are those of `flow_runs.plan_revision`, `TestSchedRelayMilestoneRefusesHandoffNotAtPinnedRevision`; answers are judged against that plan basis), so the flow does not read the head and does not follow it. The refusal raises the run-level hold `plan_superseded` and the execution a refused replay leaves is returned with it: CRW-1291.md. port: kept.
- The body names a plan digest beside the slice in the release request: `ops.ReleaseRequest.PlanDigest` carries the digest of the plan the flow compared, and the relay compares the slice only (its plan is the DAG, not the flow's milestone document), as the body's decided answer says. port: kept.

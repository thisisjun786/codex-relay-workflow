# CRW-1087 — recall lanes limited their pool before the scope filter

Source: CXC v0.2.40 (commit 3c1459ac) `plugins/codexclaw/components/recall/src/index-search.ts:291,310-342` (both bm25 lanes take a global `LIMIT` before role, source, cwd, date and synthetic filters; `candidateFilter` is applied at `:108-143`) and `recall/src/memory-search.ts:690-698` (stage1 presence); the Go side is `internal/recall/indexsearch.go`, `internal/recall/memorystage1.go` and `internal/recall/memorysearch.go`. Found by the 10-10 CXC improvement review, A8-03.

- Rows outside the scope filled the lanes' pool and pushed an older, relevant row out of it; the top-up that follows takes only the newest rows, so the loss was not repaired (with 201 main rows unchanged, adding 200 subagent rows changed the best result) (source `index-search.ts:291,310-342`); port: fixed (`indexRankLaneRanks` carries the eligibility predicate of `candidateFilter` in its own query, joined to `msgs` and `files`, ahead of the lane's `ORDER BY bm25 ... LIMIT`, so a lane's rank is the rank among eligible rows. The final semantic matcher, the bounded top-up for tokenizations the lanes do not support, the same-origin checkout rule, the tool and synthetic exclusion and the recency weight are unchanged. Pinned by `TestIndexRankScopeExcludesRowsBeforeThePoolIsCut`: for `--source`, `--role`, a cwd sibling, a same-origin checkout, a cutoff date, synthetic rows and tool logs, 600 better-matching rows outside the scope leave the eligible page as it is without them. Not changed, as the issue says: bm25's global document-frequency and average-length statistics still include the out-of-scope rows, so a score can differ from the one a scoped corpus alone would give; only candidate exclusion is prevented).
- Lane ranks are taken among eligible rows, so the fused score of a hit rises when rows the search excludes used to rank ahead of it (another source, an older date, synthetic and tool rows); order is unchanged in the recorded cases. The recorded scores of `TestChatSearchRecordedOracle` (4 cases), `TestIndexRankRecordedOracle` (`fixture/trigram/5`, `fixture/zebra/13`, `fixture/trigram/21`), the `cli__chat__search_refresh_builds_index` fixture in `TestRecallCLIRecordedCorpus`, and the contract claim of that fixture are updated to the new values; port: fixed.
- Cost: on 60,000 single-message files the lane query takes 35 ms before and 33 ms after when one row in ten is eligible, and 35 ms before and 67 ms after when every row is eligible and matches (the unicode61 lane; the trigram lane 58 ms before, 54 ms and 89 ms after). The plan scans the FTS table first and looks up `msgs` and `files` by key (`TestIndexRankLaneCost`, run with `CRW_RECALL_PERF=1`; `evidence/crw-1087-lane-cost.log`).
- Stage1 presence (which boundary terms exist anywhere, deciding whether the relaxed substring pass runs) counted rows and files the cwd scope rejects, so an out-of-scope row that held a term blocked the scoped relaxation (source `memory-search.ts:690-698`; recorded case `scope-presence-blocks-relax`); port: fixed (the tally of stage1 rows and of memory files is made after the same scope decision the search makes, `memorySearchScopeAdjust` with the thread's recorded cwd and origin key; a row or file the scope rejects adds no presence. Rows with no body hold no term and need no separate filter. Pinned by `TestMemoryStage1PresenceIsCountedInScope`; the recorded case is replaced by it).

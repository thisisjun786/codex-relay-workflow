# CRW-1211 — managed start waits for the standby turn, and one recovery model replaces unload-and-resend

Source: CRW's own design (0.5.0-alpha design issue R03), not the CXC oracle: CXC v0.2.40, commit 3c1459ac, has no managed start. The Go side is `internal/relay/managed` (`run_steps.go` `waitStandby`, `settleStandby`, `awaitStandby`; `reconcile.go` `settleOrphan`, `recreateOrphan`, `currentCreation`; `create.go`; `legacy_resend.go`), `internal/relay/adapter/host.go` (`Managed.CreateThreadHeld`) and `internal/bridge/create.go` (`CreateThreadWatched`). The documents `docs/relay/invariants.md` I-558, `docs/relay/subscriptions.md` (Recovery from other loaded MCP settings) and `docs/relay/README.md` are rewritten in the same change. The change closes CRW-1070 (an archive whose reply was lost, then another client's unarchive, then a complete archived listing that misses the child, archived the same thread a second time inside the successor budget).

- A running, missing or unknown standby turn answered `incomplete` / `standby_incomplete` on the first look and the caller had to repeat the request (`run_steps.go` `awaitStandby` read the turn once); port: fixed (the call waits for the turn inside itself, up to `Start.StandbyWait`, 60 seconds, reading the creation watch's `turn/completed` first and `ReadTurn` every `Start.StandbyPoll`, one second, otherwise; the watch is held from the creation until the wait is over and let go once, on every way out; `standby_incomplete` is answered only once the bound has passed and keeps its shape. A caller's timeout has to be longer than the bound, which `docs/relay/README.md` says. Pinned by `TestStartWaitsForStandbyCompletion`, `TestStandbyIncompleteOnlyAfterBound`, `TestWatchHeldDuringWait` and `TestReplayAfterWaitIdempotent`).
- A child the host held loaded under settings other than the request's could only be lowered by `thread/archive` then `thread/unarchive` of the registered child and the business turn sent again under a successor operation id (`business_resend.go`, about 370 lines, with begin and result `managed_resend_unloaded` rows); port: fixed (the path is deleted. A child the host reports idle with another model, reasoning effort or working directory whose history is exactly its standby turn is an orphan: it is archived once and the request creates its child again as the next creation attempt of the same request, at most `maxCreationAttempts` 3. A history with another turn, or one that cannot be read completely, is held and never archived. Pinned by `TestStandbyOnlyWrongSettingsRecreated`, `TestNonStandbyHistoryHeld` and `TestNoBusinessResendPathRemains`).
- The unload's archive could run twice for one thread (CRW-1070): after a lost reply the archived listing was asked, a complete miss was read as "not archived" and the attempt stayed available; port: fixed (the orphan archive is marked in a `managed_standby_orphan` row before the host call, an archive whose answer is lost is never run again and holds the request `lifecycle_unknown` / `standby_orphan_held`, whatever the archived listing shows, in the same call, in a repeat and after a restart; the listing is not consulted. Pinned by `TestCRW1070RaceArchivedAtMostOnce`, `TestCRW1070LostArchiveReplyRetryBounded` and `TestCRW1070OtherClientUnarchiveRace`).

Deviations the change records, none of them a CXC oracle behavior:

- The wait and the orphan judgement run after the creation and before the reservation records the child (`settleStandby`, between `checkCreation` and `recordCreation`), not at the business step, because a recorded and registered child cannot be replaced by another (`Reservation.Receipt` refuses a second child for a request). A standby that has not ended when the bound passes is not judged there: the child is registered as before and `awaitStandby` answers `standby_incomplete` with the receipt it has always had. The project's lock stays held over the wait.
- The orphan is recognised by what `thread/read` reports for the loaded child (model, reasoning effort, working directory; a field the host does not report is not a difference) and, through the adapter (`managed.MCPChecker`, `adapter.Managed.MCPProblem`), by the child's MCP servers: the profile of the child's role is resolved against the host as a resume resolves it and compared with the thread's `mcpServerStatus/list`. A standby-only child the host holds under other MCP servers is archived once and created again like one held under another model (`TestStandbyOnlyWrongMCPRecreated`, `TestMCPOnlyMismatchRecreatedOverSocket`). An answer that cannot decide (no policy, a role without profiles, an unresolvable profile, an unreadable list) leaves the child to the business guard, which still refuses it with `settings_not_preserved`.
- The wait is one deadline over the whole standby wait, the host reads included (`waitStandby`): a read still running at the bound is cut and the call answers `standby_incomplete` (the App Server client reports the cut as its own `*appserver.PhaseTimeout`, which `cutByDeadline` counts once the bound's deadline has passed; `TestStandbyBoundCutsPhaseTimeoutRead`, and over the real socket `TestManagedStandbyBoundCutsSlowReadOverSocket`), a caller's own cancellation stays the caller's error, and no re-read gets a budget of its own. The watch is asked again after every read and once more when the bound passes, so a `turn/completed` that arrived during a slow read or the poll interval is the standby's end, not `standby_incomplete` (`TestWatchCompletionInsideBoundIsKept`); a read is not cancelled for the watch, because an App Server request cancelled mid-write closes the connection that carries the watch. The turn the wait saw end (the creation watch's `turn/completed` or the host's record) is kept for the business step (`startRun.ended`), so the watch's proof is not asked of the host again; the call order of a start that goes through loses the second `ReadTurn` (`TestWatchTerminalSurvivesRelease`, `TestStandbyBoundCoversReadTurn`).
- A standby that has not ended when the bound passes cannot be judged where a new child is, and the child is recorded and registered as before (the python-capture receipts pin the registration of a `standby_incomplete` first call, and the reservation publishes one child per request, so it cannot be replaced afterwards). The call that later finds the standby ended therefore judges it without archiving (`holdRecordedOrphan`): an orphan by the rule above is held as `recipient_not_idle` / `standby_orphan_held` before any business send, and the request goes on once the host has unloaded the child. This is a deviation from the replacement an unrecorded orphan gets (`TestTimedOutStandbyIsJudgedWhenItEnds`).
- A business send that failed before `turn/start` derives no successor operation any more. A request that already holds `managed_resend_unloaded` rows keeps their meaning through a read-only reader (`legacy_resend.go`): the newest row of the business attempt decides (`legacyLowered`). Only the closing row of a lowering that archived (`ok`, `reply_lost`) and unarchived (`ok`) the child licenses the successor (replayed as recorded); a begin row without a closing row, and a closing row that recorded `archive` `none` or `unknown` or an unarchive that failed, hold as `recipient_not_idle` whatever the host reports now, since the old path would have lowered the child again or an operator was to act, and nothing archives for it now. The request, dispatch, relationship and generation identity are preserved (`TestLegacyBeginRowWithoutCloseStaysHeld`, `TestLegacyUnfinishedLoweringStaysHeld`, `TestLegacyClosingRowReplays`, `TestIdentityPreservedAcrossCollapse`).
- The call-order contract of a start that goes through gains one `thread/read` after the standby ends (the orphan judgement), and the python-capture check of the host-ready refusals counts it (`Test27_MST_8_PythonHostReadyRefusalsWholeOutput`, `TestRunCharacterization_*`).
- An orphan is replaced only when the host would let a business turn start on it (`settleOrphan`, `hostReady` on the old child before the archive): a paused, usage- or budget-limited goal, a child that takes no direct input, an archived or unknown thread holds the request at the `business` stage with that code and `standby_orphan_held` for every way the standby ended, as the business step held the child before the replacement existed (`awaitStandby`, `businessGuard`); the old child's hold is not replaced by the new child's state (`TestOrphanReplacementKeepsTheChildsHost`).
- A child that is no longer idle at the last read before the archive, or that cannot be read, holds the request `incomplete` / `recipient_not_idle` (`lifecycle_unknown` when unreadable) with `standby_orphan_held`; the child is not registered and no business turn is sent to it, so a transient busy state does not become a permanent `business_failed` and the unload recovery's retryable answer is kept (`TestOrphanBecameActiveBeforeArchiveHoldsTheRequest`).
- A host read that fails on its own while the standby bound is still open no longer hides a `turn/completed` the creation watch already holds (`waitStandby`); the error is the call's only when the watch has nothing, and the caller's cancellation stays the caller's (`TestWatchCompletionBeatsAFailedRead`).

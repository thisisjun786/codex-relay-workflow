# CRW-1389 — TestPipelineNoBarrier failed in 0.00s under load: it let item 0 go when stage 2 was asked for, not when it was recorded

Source: CRW's own test of `pipeline()` (`internal/flow/engine/engine_test.go`), not the CXC oracle (CXC v0.2.40, commit 3c1459ac): the oracle has no flow engine. Integration `cc-q10141753` (base `129aaf4b`) failed it once in its test-rest leg; a run again passed.

- The test held item 0 in stage 1 until the fake backend had been asked for the stage-2 turns of items 1 and 2, and then checked that their completed rows came before item 0's. The release came from inside the stage-2 turn of the later item, before its completed row was stored, so under load item 0's turn returned and its completed row was stored first: `stage 2 of items 1 and 2 must complete before stage 1 of item 0: map[stage1:0:30 ... stage2:1:31 stage2:2:28]` (one failure in 300 runs under five spinners on `7d2430bb`). The engine was right: `pipeline()` has no barrier, and the stage-2 rows were written once the turns returned; port: fixed (the test lets item 0 go when the journal has stored the completed rows of stage 2 of items 1 and 2, through a journal that closes a channel on those rows; a pipeline with a barrier would never run stage 2 while item 0 is in stage 1, so item 0 is let go after ten seconds and the order check fails. 20 and 300 runs under five spinners pass).

# CRW-1086 — a spent per-phase Stop budget latches for the turn

Source: CXC v0.2.40 (commit 3c1459ac) `plugins/codexclaw/components/pabcd-state/src/hook.ts` `observeProgress` (:1465-1490) and `bumpStopCounter` (:1497-1521). The Go side is `internal/pabcd/hook/stop.go` (`stopBump`, `stopObserveProgress`). Found by the 10-10 CXC improvement review (A3-03).

- On the release that ends a phase's three blocks, `bumpStopCounter` clears `stopBlockPhase`, `stopBlockWorkPhaseId` and `stopBlockCount`, and the next Stop reads the cleared phase as a phase change, so the per-phase budget recharges without any progress (hook.ts:1488,1505). Another Stop evaluator that blocks (the separately registered bg-wake leg with an undelivered completion, a completion gate) keeps the host turn alive and the host calls Stop again, so one turn at an unchanged phase drew block/block/block/release/block and 18 PABCD blocks in its first 24 Stops; port: fixed (the release keeps the phase and the work phase it was judged on and holds the count at one past the budget, so every further Stop of the same turn at the same phase and active work phase releases. Only real progress rearms it: a phase transition, a switch of the bound goalplan's active work phase, an improving metric row, or a new user turn (UserPromptSubmit's turn stamp resets the turn's total to 0, which nothing else does, so a total of 0 is the first Stop of a new turn and starts a fresh budget). `stop_hook_active` is not read: a release on it would also stop real autonomous progress. The other Stop evaluators' budgets are unchanged, and the turn's total of 24 and its one announcement are unchanged. Pinned by `TestCRW1086PhaseCapLatchHoldsWhileAnotherEvaluatorContinues` (a fake second evaluator keeps blocking: three PABCD blocks, then empty answers until the total cap's announcement), `TestCRW1086RealProgressRearmsTheLatch` (each of the four rearms) and `TestCRW1086ReleasesStillReleaseWhileLatched` (pause, approval wait, compaction recovery and a stale turn release without a write and leave the latch); `TestStopStallReleasesAfterThreeBlocksPerPhase` and `TestStopNeverTransitionsTheCycle` now expect the latched state. The recorded fixture `hook__stop-checking-pabcd-continuation__stop_budget_three_per_phase` keeps its four answers; its final state keeps `stopBlockPhase` A with `stopBlockCount` 4, claimed as intentionally changed in `contract/notes/cxc/CRW-1086.json`. Red output: `/state/crw/crw-lane-pabcd-stop/evidence/red-1086.log`).

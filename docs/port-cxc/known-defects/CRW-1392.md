# CRW-1392 — session engine P3s after CRW-1367, CRW-1362 and CRW-1278: the adoption's lost path, a continuation's effort, a stray ref that never moved, a step that never wrote, the tokens of a held call, a budget refusal, and three tests that depended on order, time or a shared home

Source: CRW's own design (CRW-1316 design sections 3, 5 and 6.3; CRW-1278 and CRW-1327 for the budget), not the CXC oracle (CXC v0.2.40, commit 3c1459ac): the oracle has no flow engine. The items are the P3s the gradings and verifications of lanes S5 (CRW-1362+1367) and b2 (CRW-1278+1327) left outside their scope, and the comments of CRW-1367 and CRW-1392. The Go side is `internal/flow/engine` (`session.go`: `sessionAdopt`, `sessionBind`, `stepUnwritten` new; `call.go`: `planResume`; `sessionhost.go`: `HeadKeeper`), `internal/flow/backend/agent/git.go` (`Allocator.KeepRef`) and `internal/flow/cli/resume.go` (`runBudget`, `moreBudget` new).

- The adoption of a recorded subagent (a process died after `thread_started`, `sessionAdopt`) bound the next attempt right after it lost the attempt, without the gate the harvest's lost path passes, so a run paused at that moment published the next attempt's step; port: fixed (the gate decides the next attempt first. Pinned by `TestSessionAdoptLossPassesTheGate`: no step of attempt 2, the call held `engine_paused`).
- The adoption started its lost timer only when the host did not know the subagent; a thread the host still read and showed `notLoaded` or `systemError`, with no marker turn it could list, was answered `retry` until the turn's context ended; port: fixed (the harvest's rule, `hostGone`: unknown, `notLoaded` or `systemError` for `LostAfter` loses the attempt, a read the host cannot answer judges nothing and leaves the time the subagent has been gone as it is, and `session_attempt_lost` records the status the host showed as `host_status`. Pinned by `TestSessionAdoptLosesAGoneSubagentAfterLostAfter` and `TestSessionAdoptKeepsTheGoneTimeOverAFailedRead`, whose host fails every third read of the gone subagent).
- The `LostAfter` interval of the adoption was pinned by no test: losing at the first read that showed the subagent gone passed the `recorded subagent gone` case; port: fixed (the cases of `TestSessionAdoptLosesAGoneSubagentAfterLostAfter` measure the lost row against the first read that showed the subagent gone, notFound among them, and want at least `LostAfter`).
- No test held that the App Server path compares a continuation's whole pair (only a session run compares the decided parts): dropping the `e.cfg.Session != nil` condition of `planResume` passed the package; port: fixed (`TestAppServerContinuationComparesTheWholePair`: a thread read back on another effort than the role's undecided one is refused and so is its continuation. Its mutation run, the condition dropped, fails: `results [refused pair_refused completed <nil>]`).
- `TestSessionContinuationOnADecidedPairThatDiffersIsRefused` caught a continuation that was not refused only by the run's 20 second timeout; port: fixed (the run is ended when the continuation publishes a step and the test says the continuation was not refused; its mutation run, the refusal dropped, fails in 0.02s with `the continuation was not refused: it published 1 step(s)`).
- An explicit `effort` on a continuation whose role leaves the effort undecided was compared with the empty effort and refused even when it was the effort the host applied; port: fixed (in a session run it is compared with the effort the call's subagent ran, the configured pair the bind recorded with `effort_unchecked`. Pinned by `TestSessionContinuationEffortOfAnUndecidedRole`: the applied effort continues, another is refused `pair_refused` naming the thread's effort. The App Server path binds a thread only on the role's whole pair and keeps the refusal: `TestAppServerContinuationEffortOfAnUndecidedRoleIsRefused`).
- The second `HEAD` of one call (its later turn after a crash) was only a `ref_error`: `KeepRef` refused to move a stray ref; port: fixed (decided default: a commit that descends from the commit the ref holds moves it by a fast-forward, `git update-ref <ref> <new> <old>`; a commit beside or behind the record is refused as before. Pinned by `TestAllocatorKeepRefFastForwards`; `TestAllocatorKeepRefNeverOverwrites` now refuses a commit with no history in common with the record, because its second commit descends from the first and is the fast-forward).
- A step the bus could not write for good (EACCES on the steps directory) was written again at the poll with one `step_unwritten` row at the first failure and nothing after; port: fixed (fail open as before; the row is written again at the 10th, 100th, ... failure with `failures`, `first_at` and `last_at`, and a closing row comes whenever a failure row was written: `written: false` when the turn ends with the step unwritten, `written: true` when the step is written, after any number of failures, a power of ten among them. Pinned by `TestSessionStepBusPermanentFailureIsCounted`, whose cases write the step after 25, exactly 10 and 1 failures or never; `TestSessionStepBusWriteFailureIsNotDispatched` has its first row and the closing row of its three failures).
- The token baseline of a held session call that goes on in place (b2 P2: `s.seen` set back to zero would count the tokens before the hold in the held row and again in the last row): not reproduced. A held call whose turn was delivered goes on with a new subagent (`sessionResumeHeld` sets `s.agent` to none and spawns), whose rollout counts from zero, and a call whose step was not delivered counted no tokens; `sessionAdopt` sets the baseline to zero only for a subagent spawned for the attempt's step. `TestSessionHeldCallCountsItsTokensOnce` (15 tokens before the hold, 20 after, the run's figure 35 by the journal and by the resumed process) passes on `7d2430bb` and on this lane's head: `--- PASS: TestSessionHeldCallCountsItsTokensOnce`.
- A run whose budget the operator gave (`crw flow start --token-budget`, no decision) and spent was refused with "more budget needs a new decision", which is the night batch's way only; port: fixed (decided default: the refusal stays, and for a budget without a decision it says that no command raises a run's budget and that more tokens need a new run, `crw flow start WORKFLOW --token-budget N`; a night batch's still says it needs a new decision. Pinned by `TestRunBudgetRefusalNamesAWay`).
- The second `budget_warning` of a resumed run (item 5 of the issue) was fixed by CRW-1278's second fix round (`4af66cb81`, the run starts warned when its journal holds a warning row), before this lane.
- `TestNextHandsOutTheStepsOfTheBus` failed from its second run at `-count>1`: the owner's binding is a file of the home's flow-bound directory, which the package's isolated home keeps between runs, so the missing-binding warning the test checks was not written again; port: fixed (the test runs in a home of its own).
- `TestSessionRunDeathAtEachRow`'s `runDeath` swapped the fake orchestrator's hooks (`answer`, `onSpawn`, `onInput`) while its goroutine read them (a data race of the test only); port: fixed (the orchestrator reads them under its lock, and `runDeath` swaps them under it; `go test -race` reports no race. The race run sets `-gcflags=all=-d=checkptr=0`: the SQLite cases otherwise stop in `modernc.org/libc`'s `Xmemcpy` with a checkptr fault of the race build, an environment fault of that library).
- `TestClientUserMessageIdMatchedFirst` (`internal/flow/backend/agent`) sent its two turn/start requests on one thread back to back; a thread takes one turn at a time, so when the first had not ended yet (load) the host refused the second (`activeTurnNotSteerable`) and the test failed in 0.01s, as in integration `cc-q10141933`; port: fixed (the first turn ends after a delay, so the order is exercised every run, and the test waits until the host lists it ended before the second request).
- `TestDesignLoopCapsWarnAndTellTheOrchestrator`'s comment (`internal/flow/scenario/upward_test.go`) named `budget.total` as the budget the token cap is taken from; it is `budget.run`, the budget the run's first process started with (CRW-1327); port: fixed (the comment).
- Supersedes CRW-1205.md, item beginning "`crw flow resume RUN SCRIPT` is the engine's command": its sentence that the command "refuses a `--token-budget` of 0" no longer holds; a `--token-budget` of 0 is the remainder of the spec's budget, as the flag's help says (CRW-1278, `27522338`, `specLimits`), and below 0 or above the spec's budget is refused.
- Supersedes CRW-1278.md, item beginning "Supersedes CRW-1205.md": the refusal of a run with nothing left "says that more budget is a new decision" only for a night batch; for a budget the operator gave it says that no command raises it and names a new run (above).
- Supersedes CRW-1367.md, item beginning "The stray ref of an executor that committed": `Allocator.KeepRef` no longer refuses every move; it moves a ref by a fast-forward to a descendant of its record and refuses any other move (above).
- Supersedes CRW-1367.md, item beginning "A step whose packet or file could not be written": `step_unwritten` is no longer written once; it is written at the 1st, 10th, 100th, ... failure and closed as above.

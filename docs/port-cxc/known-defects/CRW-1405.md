# CRW-1405 — e2a follow-ups: KeepRef's untested refusals, a turn a stop interrupted between two reads, a held call's repair before its repair step

Source: CRW's own design (CRW-1316 design sections 3, 5 and 6.3), not the CXC oracle (CXC v0.2.40, commit 3c1459ac): the oracle has no flow engine. The items are the three P3s the V5 review of the e2a lane (CRW-1384+1392+1389, head `377e06fe`) confirmed. The Go side is `internal/flow/backend/agent/git.go` (`Allocator.KeepRef`, unchanged) and `internal/flow/engine/session.go` (`sessionStop`, `stopStep` and `sessionStopBinds` new, `sessionBind`, `sessionAdopt`, `sessionHarvest`, `sessionResumeHeld`). The tests are `TestAllocatorKeepRefRefusesAMovedRecordAndANonCommit` (`internal/flow/backend/agent/stray_test.go`), `TestSessionStopBindsEveryTurnItInterrupts` (`internal/flow/engine/session_1405_test.go`) and `TestSessionHeldBeforeTheRepairStepHasNoRepairUsed` (`internal/flow/engine/session_1405_held_test.go`).

- `KeepRef` (CRW-1392) refuses a fast-forward over a record that moved between its read of the ref and its `update-ref` (the compare-and-swap is against the commit it read), and refuses over an existing ref a commit that names no commit, but no test reached either branch; port: fixed (tests only, `git.go` unchanged: a second writer moves the ref between the read and the update through `AllocatorConfig.Git`, and the refusal keeps the other writer's record; an object id the repository does not hold, a tree, a name that is no object and an empty name are refused as "no commit" with no `update-ref` sent. The two mutation runs, the compare-and-swap's old value dropped and the no-commit case dropped, fail the test).
- `sessionStop` read the subagent's turns again after the bind read of `sessionBindStopped` (and, on the path of a step the bus could not write, with no bind read at all) and interrupted every running turn it found, so a `send_input` turn that started between the two reads, or while the step was unwritten, was interrupted with no `turn_started`; a process that died right after the interrupt left the resumed process to bind the turn by its marker and hold the call `host_interrupted_external` instead of the `engine_paused` its `interrupt_requested` row asked for; port: fixed (every turn a stop interrupts is bound first and stored before the interrupt is sent, whichever path reached the stop: the running turns are read once, the bound turn and a running turn that carries the marker of the step the stop came to (`stopStep`, from both stops of `sessionBind` and from `sessionAdopt`; none from the harvest, whose turn is bound) are the turns it interrupts, the latter bound first through `sessionVerify` with the `conversation_turn` and `replaces_turn` its step recorded, and the journal is flushed after those binds and before the first interrupt. A running turn the stop cannot bind - no row of the call names it and it carries no marker of the step - is not interrupted: the engine never sent it, and a resumed process would read its end as an interrupt nobody asked for. Pinned by `TestSessionStopBindsEveryTurnItInterrupts`, whose host starts the repair turn at the stop's second read of the turns (after the bind read) or at its first (the step unwritten), over the memory and the SQLite journal: the process dies right after the interrupt, and the resumed call is held `engine_paused` with the task turn at conversation turn 1 and the repair turn at 2, each bound once).
- `sessionResumeHeld` took the held row's `repair_used` before it read whether the call's rows hold a repair step, so a call held at the gate of its repair turn - its task turn's answer failed validation and a pause or the budget stopped it before the repair's `step_published` - went on with the task turn on a new subagent (`firstK` = k) while it counted its one repair as used, and the completed row of a task that passed said `repair_used: true` with no repair; port: fixed (`repairUsed` is carried over where the repair step is found, `h.repairUsed && repairStep != nil`; the held repair that was published goes on as the repair, as before (CRW-1384, `TestSessionHeldRepairGoesOnAsTheRepair`). Pinned by `TestSessionHeldBeforeTheRepairStepHasNoRepairUsed`: held `budget_exhausted` after the task turn failed validation, the resumed task that passes completes with `repair_used: false` and no repair step, and one that fails again gets the call's one repair and completes with `repair_used: true`).

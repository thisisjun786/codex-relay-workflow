# CRW-1201 — project-lane.js: the outcome state machine, the crw.* operations and the scenario harness

Source: CRW's own design (0.5.0-alpha, issue F08), not the CXC oracle: CXC v0.2.40 (commit 3c1459ac) has no flow engine, so there is no oracle line. The Go side is `internal/flow/ops/{lane,lane_design,lane_decisions,lane_build,lane_land,lane_halt,lane_close,journalq,seams,decisions,premerge,verify_ci,rules,schemas}.go` and `internal/flow/workflows/{workflows.go,project-lane.js,lib/lane.js,lib/evidence.js,lib/packets.js,lib/schemas.js}`; the rule is written in `docs/flow/script.md`, "The project lane". The tests are `internal/flow/scenario/*_test.go` (the harness is `lab_test.go`, `relay_test.go`) and `internal/flow/ops/{lane,schemas}_test.go`.

- The engine registers an operation as an `engine.Op`, which cannot call an agent, and the body's `crw.review`, `crw.land`, `crw.dag`, `crw.explore` and `crw.tierStep` each combine agent calls with records. They are therefore composites in JavaScript (`internal/flow/workflows/lib/lane.js`, installed on `crw` by `install(crw)`), and the host-side halves are operations: the record of a review is the operation `crw.review` (the composite calls it after the reviewer call), `crw.land` is `crw.accept` then `crw.integrate`, and the step decision of `crw.tierStep` is minted by the engine's call and recorded by `crw.stepRecord`. The body's edit regions do not name `internal/flow/engine/`, and nothing in it changed: a composite takes no position of its own and every position it uses is that of a call or an operation, so the resume walk replays it like any other code. port: kept.
- `crw.reviewHalt` does not re-ask a `null_model` reviewer: the composite `crw.review` asks the fresh reviewer thread once more itself, so the lane is the one of the design (`if (rv.state !== 'verdict') return crw.reviewHalt(node, rv)`) and `reviewHalt` escalates a second `null_model`. The calls and the states are the body's. port: kept.
- A verdict the Go side decodes (`ops.DecodeVerdict`) follows two rules the JSON Schema cannot say: a defect of kind `evidence_missing` carries its `evidence_question` (the body), and a `verified` verdict names no unmet criterion and no defect of P0 to P2 (the body does not say: those are the findings a premerge record needs a disposition for, and the default disposition is `blocking`, so a verified verdict over one would be refused at the acceptance). An answer that breaks a rule is `null_model` with reason `verdict_invalid` and gets the one second reviewer thread. port: kept.
- The `premerge` disposition default is `blocking` and nothing else: `ops.AssemblePremerge` disposes every finding the judgment asks a disposition for as blocking, and a head whose record the judgment refuses is `land_refused` with the closed reason (`premerge_blocked`); the lane escalates it. `separable` and `carried` are the orchestrator's, through the escalation (the decision path of F12), and no code of this issue writes them. port: kept.
- A refusal of `crw.accept` or `crw.integrate` is an operation error that carries a closed reason (`refused:<reason>:`), so the row is a refusal row and not a completed row: the engine's accepted-node check (`nodeAccepted`) and `ops.AcceptProof` read completed `crw.accept` rows only, and a resume asks again. port: kept.
- The design audit (added to the body by the coherence check of 10-11) is `crw.designAudit` (the record, the state `approved` or `needs_changes` or the call's own status) and the rule id `SP-AUD-1` in `ops/rules.go`, which the spawn policy does not list; the audit is a structural spawn of a reviewer, and the ledger and the release refuse a DESIGN with no approved audit, so no executor can start without one. The auditor's model family is checked against the architect's (`independence_refused`, `independence_unproven`) the way a review is. port: kept.
- The design ledger holds revision `r0` only, as the body says: `crw.designLedger` refuses any other revision with a message that names the re-plan issue (F09), and `crw.issuePlan` reads the ledger row of the revision the node's release row names, so a revision the re-plan issue appends later changes no lane that started under `r0`. port: pending (F09).
- `crw.milestoneClose` and `crw.reportUp` are the seams of the issues that follow (`ops.Closer`: the final gate and reviewer-final of F13; `ops.Reporter`: the delivery of F11). This issue records the call, the range and the tip it judged, and a close whose recorded tip is not the integration target any more runs live again; the gate, the QA receipt and the review are not here. port: pending (F13, F11).
- `ops.Relay` is the interface of the scheduler the lane uses (the nodes of a milestone, the agent release, the agent acceptance and integration of R05, `AgentClose` of R07, the issue child). The scenario harness implements it with a fake over a temporary repository; the implementation over `dagsched.Scheduler` is not in this issue, because a release request needs the host settings, the rule version and the artifact roots only the run process holds (the library functions of `dagsched` have no caller until the run process, R10 and F12, is written). port: pending (R10, F12).
- `ops.CILocalVerifier` runs `crw-dev ci local --commit <head> --record <file> --runner flow` in the node's worktree and reads the record it leaves; it is tested with a command runner of the test, not against the real `crw-dev ci local`, which runs the whole CI. A command that exits 0 over a record that says fail, a record with no digest, or no record at all is not a pass. port: kept.
- `crw.dag` is a composite on `parallel` with its own gates (the order of the nodes the scheduler offers, the nodes a node waits for, `cap`, region overlap), not an engine join; the signal paths of F09 need the engine to open paths of their own and are not here. A node the scheduler holds back (no slot, no memory) is `unreleased` in the run's result and is asked again by the next resume (the row of a release that did not happen is never replayed). port: pending (F09 for the signal path).
- Rule ids: `NativeRule` is `RuleNat1`, so `ops/rules.go` is the one file of `internal/flow` that writes an `SP-` string (`TestRuleIDsHaveOneSource` reads the sources); scripts name a rule by its key (`EXEC_1`, `TIER_1`). port: kept.
- The bodies of F08 and the design file differ in the count of the end-condition tests (22 and 25 in the first command); the 25 named tests of the Linear body, the 6 and the 2 of the other two commands are all run by `-v` and each shows `=== RUN` and `--- PASS`. port: kept.
- Four files outside the body's edit regions change with this issue and only because of what the regions hold: `internal/flow/script/load_test.go` lists the exports of `crw:lib/schemas` (it gains `DESIGN_AUDIT`), `testdata/flow/shadow/trace.golden.json` records the digest of the helper `crw:lib/schemas` (regenerated with `CRW_UPDATE_GOLDEN=1`; nothing else in it moved), the comment of `engine.tierStep` in `internal/flow/engine/tierstep.go` (words only: the evidence rules and the step row are the lane's, not a later issue's), and `docs/flow/script.md` and `docs/flow/journal.md` (the operations and the rows). `ops.recordOps` (`proofs.go`) gains the recording operations of the lane (`spawnDecision`, `stepRecord`, `exploreRoute`, `exploreRecord`, `designAudit`, `designLedger`), so a recorded one is never sent again. port: kept.
- A release is proven on a resume by the journal when the run itself ended the execution, not only by `Relay.ReleaseStands`: a later completed `crw.escalate` row of the node with `closed: true` (the node was given up as `tiers_exhausted`, `blocked_scope` or `size_split` and the relay closed the execution) or a later completed `crw.integrate` row of the node proves the release. A closed execution does not stand at the relay, so without this a resume released the given-up node again and built it from a fresh executor, ignoring the escalation. `ReleaseStands` is documented as true while the execution is open and for a node that landed, false for a closed or re-released execution; `FakeRelay.Close` now ends the execution as that says (`TestResumeAfterGivenUpNodeRunsNothing`). port: kept.
- The end of the tiers is recorded with its rule: before the `tiers_exhausted` escalation the lane writes an `SP-TIER-2` row (decision `refused`, kind `signal`, the evidence of the tier) in `flow_spawn_decisions`. port: kept.
- `crw.verify` passes the integration tip as the base of the verification (`VerifyRequest.Base`), so `crw-dev ci local --base` names the range the acceptance judges and not the merge-base with `origin/dev`. port: kept.

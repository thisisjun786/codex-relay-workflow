# CRW-1126 — a failed helper's fallback waits for its recorded child to be seen ending

Source: `plugins/codexclaw/components/subagent-config/src/fallback-dispatch.ts` at v0.2.40 (commit 3c1459ac), `report`
(:169-205, the stopped handoff at :189-204) and `taskFailed` (:216-234, the handoff at :233); the Go port's
`dispatchReport`, `dispatchTaskFailed` and `dispatchHandoff` in `internal/role/dispatch_ledger.go`, the checked boundary
`CheckedDispatch` and the stopped close `createdCheckStop` in `internal/role/created_check.go`, the CLI host opener
`dispatchChecked` in `internal/role/dispatch_cmd.go`, and the new shared observation in `internal/role/dispatch_observe.go`.

- A `failed` or `task_failed` report of an attempt whose child is recorded hands the attempt on (a ready fallback attempt, or
  `main-direct` on the last candidate) on the caller's `executionState: "stopped"`, matching `agentId` and non-empty
  `reconciliation` alone; nothing reads the child, so a child that is still active or has a turn in progress is replaced by
  a second writer, or by the main session, on a false or early stop claim (source `fallback-dispatch.ts:189-204` and
  `:229-233`; the Go checked boundary sent these reports to the parity ledger unchanged, and the CLI opened its read-only
  host only for the stopped close); port: fixed (the checked boundary asks the shared termination observation before the
  handoff: the child's identity and thread status from the App Server, its newest turn from the App Server's turn list, and,
  for a native helper the App Server cannot see, from the child's rollout, the host's own record of its turns
  (`task_complete`/`turn_aborted` after the newest `task_started`). A child of another parent, an active thread and a newest
  turn in progress refuse and write nothing; a thread reported active whose newest turn ended, a host that cannot answer and
  a turn that was not seen to end leave the attempt `reconcile` with its child id kept; a newest turn that completed, was
  interrupted or failed hands on once and records the observation on the attempt as `termination`, which is a snapshot and
  not a reservation. The CLI opens its host for failed and task_failed reports too. A failure with no recorded child
  (`not_created`) reads nothing, and the parity ledger `RunDispatch` keeps the oracle behaviour. The stopped close reads
  the child through the same observation and keeps its own rules, including closing with "runtime not confirmed" when
  the end could not be seen. Every non-empty rollout line is decoded and classified by its decoded `type`, never by the text it
  contains; a line that does not parse, terminated by a newline or not (damage, a write in progress, a truncated tail), is
  not skipped: what was read before it no longer shows how the newest
  turn stands, so the end stays unseen (reconcile, or an unconfirmed cleanup) until a later turn starts and ends whole.
  Pinned by `TestDispatchHandoffDamagedRolloutDoesNotShowAnEnd`, `TestDispatchCleanupDamagedRolloutStaysUnconfirmed`,
  `TestDispatchHandoffRequiresTheRecordedChildToHaveEnded`,
  `TestDispatchHandoffOnceAfterATerminalTurn`, `TestDispatchHandoffWithoutAChildAsksNothing`,
  `TestDispatchHandoffNativeRolloutEvidence` and `TestDispatchCommandHandoffReadsTheHost`).
- The rollout is evidence only through the file that was looked at (found by the pre-merge evaluation of this change; the rollout
  reader is new code of this issue, with no oracle behaviour to keep): the path is looked at with `Lstat` and opened without
  following a link and without blocking, and the opened descriptor must be the regular file that was looked at
  (`os.SameFile`), as the ledger's own record reader requires, for both rollout readers (the child's newest turn and the
  parent's spawn-result read of CRW-1127). A path replaced in between by a link to another rollout, another regular file
  or a named pipe shows nothing (the end stays unseen) instead of another rollout's terminal turn, and a named pipe cannot
  block the open while the record lock is held. Pinned by `TestDispatchRolloutIsTheFileThatWasLookedAt`. A rollout scan is
  not yet bounded by the observation's deadline or a line size; that is carried to a follow-up.

# CRW-1118 — a refused spawn no longer spends a subagent's one-time recursion grant

Source: CXC v0.2.40 (commit 3c1459ac) `plugins/codexclaw/components/subagent-config/src/spawn-attach-hook.ts:392-406`
(`consumeRecursionGrant`) and :882 (its call before the managed dispatch, the role settings, the final-gate check and the answer);
the Go port's `spawnGrantCheck`, `spawnGrantClaim` (`reserve`, `commit`, `release`) and `spawnGrantUnexpired` in
`internal/role/spawn/grant.go`, `spawnHookAssembleWith` in `internal/role/spawn/hook.go`, and `spawnHookAssembly.finish` and
`RunSpawnAttachHook` in `internal/role/spawn/hook_route.go`. Found by the 10-10 CXC improvement review (A5-05).

- The oracle spends a subagent's grant (a rename, then a removal) before any other check of the spawn, so a spawn it then refuses
  for a deterministic reason (a managed marker with no dispatch record, a final-gate packet without its receipts) has spent the
  capability and the corrected retry is refused with LEAF-TOPOLOGY (source :882 against :892-907 and :1072-1079); port: fixed (the
  hook checks the grant without spending it, runs every refusal, writes its answer, and only then reserves the grant (a rename,
  which only one call can make), issues a managed attempt, and spends the grant; a refusal after the reservation gives it back. An
  answer that lets the spawn run untouched spends it too, and a failure after the spend is denied, never let through. Pinned by
  `TestSpawnHookRefusedSpawnKeepsTheGrantForTheRetry` and `TestSpawnHookGrantRaceAllowsOneCall`).
- The oracle's spent grant is anonymous, so a second delivery of the same native call is refused while nothing ties a reservation
  to the call that made it; port: fixed (with the hook's `tool_use_id` the reservation and the spent record are named after a digest
  of the id: the same call finds its own record and is answered again the same way, another call finds nothing to use, and a
  reservation left by a delivery that stopped before it committed is finished by that call only. Without a tool use id the
  reservation has a unique name and the spent grant is removed, as before. Pinned by
  `TestSpawnHookRefusedSpawnKeepsTheGrantForTheRetry` and `TestSpawnHookStaleReservationAuthorizesOnlyItsCall`).
- Supersedes known-defects.md, section "Found by the CRW-612 spawn recursion grant port", item beginning "`consumeRecursionGrant`
  accepts any JSON number as `expiresAt`": now port: fixed (`expiresAt`, the last of a repeated key, must be a finite integer of
  milliseconds not before now and not more than the 15-minute TTL after it, which is what the minting hook writes; a fraction, an
  overflow to infinity and a far-future value are refused, and the refused grant is removed as the oracle removes one it reads.
  Pinned by `TestSpawnHookGrantExpiryMustBeAFiniteIntegerWithinTheTTL`; the recorded planted bodies whose `expiresAt` is
  1900000000000, 1900000000000.5 or 1e999 are replayed by `TestGrantOraclePlantedBodies` as intentionally changed).
- Supersedes known-defects.md, section "Found by the CRW-612 spawn recursion grant port", item beginning "A consumed grant is
  renamed to": now port: kept with new names (a call that dies between the reservation and the commit leaves
  `<digest>.json.reserved-<tag>`, which only its own call can finish, and a spent grant of a call with a tool use id stays as
  `<digest>.json.used-<tag>` so the same delivery is answered again; nothing sweeps either, as nothing sweeps unused grants; they
  live in the 0700 per-user key directory under the temp root).

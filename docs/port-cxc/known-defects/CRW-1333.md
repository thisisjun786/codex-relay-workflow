# CRW-1333 — one management seat for the whole store, no management -> lead record, and no channel for a lead's message upward

Source: CRW's own design (CRW-1314 §1 and §2, lane D1a), not the CXC oracle (CXC v0.2.40, commit 3c1459ac): the oracle has the store seat (`--scope-kind store`) as the only supervisor scope above an initiative and a supervisor channel whose recipient is the initiative supervisor or that seat. The Go side is `internal/relay/registry` (`rolepolicy.go` `bindingScopeKind`, `linkage.go` `bindingRefusal`, `linkage_write.go` `bindScopeKind`, `retireStoreSeat`, `managementEdgePlan`, `HandoverScopeKind`), `internal/relay/capacity/capacity.go` (`checkDeclarer`), `internal/manage/supervisor.go` (register and show), `internal/relay/supervisor` (`hierarchy.go` `Resolve`, `resolve_direct.go`, `direct.go`, `send.go` `render`) and `internal/relay/cli/reportup.go`. Found by the 10-13 field trial (I-36, P-CRW-140; CRW-1315 40, D4, D8, L7): the lead asked the management session for a release-gate exception and went idle, and nothing could wake the management session, which was not on the store seat and had no pump running.

- Management sessions had one seat between them (the store seat, one key, one live owner), so a second session could not register without displacing the first; port: fixed (`linkage-bind --role supervisor --scope-kind management --scope <seat id>` binds a seat per session, keyed by the thread that first registered it, with `task_id` its current owner; `crw manage supervisor register` binds the calling thread's seat (`--task`, else `CODEX_THREAD_ID`, else the configuration's supervisor section, which stays the fallback; `--host`, else the section) and records the settings pair as before; a session that inherited a seat by handover is answered with that seat when it registers its own again; `linkage-handover --scope-kind management` moves a seat with its record (old binding archived and superseded, `handover_note`, `scope_handover`, the edge's `upper_task_id`). Pinned by `TestManagementSeatPerThread`, `TestManagementSeatHandoverIsRecorded`, `TestSupervisorRegisterBindsTheCallingThreadsManagementSeat`, `TestSupervisorRegisterTakesTheTaskAndHostFromItsArguments`, `TestSupervisorShowReadsTheManagementSeat`).
- A thread holding the store seat could not take a management seat (`role_already_bound`); port: fixed (the store seat is laid down in the same transaction, archived with `superseded_by` naming the management binding and a `scope_seat_moved` journal row; a management seat speaks for the store scope in `limit-declare`; `registry.StoreScopeSupervisor` reads a store seat laid down this way through the live owner of the management seat that took it over, so a user-grade `decision-answer`, the `jun_decision_request` recipient and the store-seat fallbacks keep finding the manager, and its successor after a handover). Pinned by `TestManagementSeatRetiresTheStoreSeatOfTheSameThread`, `TestManagementSeatLimitDeclareSucceeds`, `TestStoreScopeSupervisorFollowsTheSeatThatTookItOver`, `TestStoreScopeSupervisorWithoutASuccessorIsEmpty`, `TestDecisionAnswerAfterTheManagerRegistersItsManagementSeat`, `TestJunDecisionReportFindsTheManagerAfterItRegistersItsManagementSeat`, `TestStoreRecipient_FollowsTheManagementSeatThatTookTheSeatOver`, `TestDirectResolveInitiativeFallsBackToTheManagerAfterItRegisters`.
- Nothing recorded which management session a lead belongs to; port: fixed (`linkage-bind --role supervisor --scope <INIT> --creator <thread>`, default the caller's `CODEX_THREAD_ID`, writes the execution edge management -> initiative in the lead's transaction when the creator holds a live management seat, answered under `managementLink`; a second session's registration is refused `link_conflict` and commits only its conflict row, so an initiative changes management only by the seat's handover). Pinned by `TestLeadRegistrationWritesTheManagementEdge`, `TestLeadRegistrationSecondManagementTakesOnlyByHandover`.
- The supervisor channel could only address a relationship's or a project's report to the initiative supervisor or the store seat, with a parent-to-supervisor envelope; port: fixed (`Resolve` reads `initiative:<key>` (the lead to the management seat above it, the single store seat when no edge stands) and `run:<id>` (the run to `flow_runs.actor_task_id`); `StageDirect` stages the direct kinds of the design (`lead_report`, `lead_decision_request`, `lead_blocked`, `jun_decision_request`, the six `flow_*` kinds through `project:<key>`, `session_step_waiting` through `run:<id>`; never `blocked` or `decision_request`) the way `StageNotice` stages a fault notice; the packet names its layer and both roles and carries the text, and the rendered header names the layer and the sender; an issue's or a project's report still goes to the lead). Pinned by `TestDirectResolveInitiativeReachesTheManagementSeat`, `TestDirectResolveInitiativeFallsBackToTheStoreSeat`, `TestDirectResolveRunReachesItsOwner`, `TestDirectStageWritesOneMessagePerReport`, `TestDirectStageRefusesKindsItDoesNotStage`, `TestDirectStageReaddressesAnUnsentMessage`).
- A lead had no command to report upward; port: fixed (`crw relay report-up --initiative KEY --kind lead_report|lead_decision_request|lead_blocked` writes a `lead_report` journal row and stages the direct message in one transaction; with nobody above to tell the row is still written and the refusal says the report stands; a caller that is not the lead is not refused, it is named in the message, recorded as `reporterIsLead: false` and warned). Pinned by `TestReportUpStagesTheLeadsDecisionRequestToItsManagement`, `TestReportUpWithNobodyAboveKeepsTheReport`, `TestReportUpByAnotherThreadIsStagedWithAWarningAndARecord`, and end to end over the scripted App Server by `TestManagementSeatE2ETwoSessionsEachHearOnlyTheirOwnLead`, `TestManagementSeatE2EHandoverMovesTheSeat`, `TestManagementSeatE2EStoreSeatFallback`.
- Deviation: `crw manage supervisor register` binds a management seat, not the store seat, so its argument vector and help text changed (the CRW-780/921 tests pin the new ones); the relay store's schema is unchanged (new values in `scope_bindings.scope_kind`, `scope_links.upper_kind`, `supervisor_messages.obligation_kind` and the `initiative:`/`run:` relationship prefixes, none of which carries a CHECK; `relay_schema_shipped.json` and its compat test are untouched); the flow side (a `RelaySender` replacing `ManageSender`, `report_delivery` settled by the daemon), the busy and idle-edge wake of supervisor messages, the decision authority over several seats (the store seat read through its successor is the single seat's rule carried over) and the skill procedures are lanes D2, D1b, D3 and D4 of CRW-1314; port: kept (reversible code and row values only, Jun 10-13: warn and record, fail open).
- Deviation (CRW-1486/S4): the store seat read through the management seat that took it over answers only for a user-grade message, a decision the user owes (`answerOwedBy` `user`: `decision_request`, `jun_decision_request`, `flow_jun_decision_request`, a fault notice of kind `decision`); every other message of a project or an initiative with no management edge and no live store seat above it is refused `unregistered_scope` at staging and held `hierarchy_unresolved` at its next claim if it was staged before, instead of reaching the manager, so a parent that registered does not hear every other project's notifications (`internal/relay/supervisor/grade.go`, `hierarchy.go` `ResolveFor` and `withStoreSeat`, `resolve_direct.go`, `notice_project.go`; one grade per message at staging, claim, transport fence, reopen and `ReportHolds`; `faults.NoticeChannel.Resolve` takes the grade). A live store seat folds for every grade, and `registry.StoreScopeSupervisor`, the decision-answer authority, the capacity declarer and the flow side's Jun-decision recipient are unchanged. The two pins above now expect `unregistered_scope` for a notification and the manager for a user decision: `TestStoreRecipient_FollowsTheManagementSeatThatTookTheSeatOver`, `TestDirectResolveInitiativeFallsBackToTheManagerAfterItRegisters`; the new rule is pinned by `TestSuccessorStoreSeatDoesNotHearOtherProjects`, `TestStagedMessageToSuccessorIsHeldUnaddressed`, `TestSuccessorCarriesAUserGradeMessageThroughEveryStep`, `TestOwnEscalationIsTheHeadAfterTheHandover` and the `grade_test.go` table tests. Port: kept (reversible resolution only, no stored format changes).

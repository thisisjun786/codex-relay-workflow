No defect was found in this slice. The budget guard, the interview guard and the fail-closed dispatcher
reproduce the oracle's texts and decisions byte for byte, and the three recorded fixtures that exercise them
replay identical. The two behaviours that read as odd are the oracle's design and are ported as they are,
so neither is a defect of the port:

- A create_goal carrying any key beside objective is denied, token_budget included, so the host's own
  budgeted goal cannot be created while this hook is installed (goal-gate.ts:105-125); port: kept, because
  the guard exists to keep the goal unlimited and the deny text says so.
- A payload that does not parse is passed through rather than denied, so only a failure after the parse
  reaches the fail-closed deny (goal-gate.ts:316-324); port: kept, because the oracle's own test asserts the
  pass-through (goal-gate.test.ts:260-262) and the issue's parity rule wins.

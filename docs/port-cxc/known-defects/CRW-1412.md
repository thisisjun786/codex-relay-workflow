# CRW-1412 — a stop that reads two running turns of one step

Source: CRW's own design, a follow-up of CRW-1409 (a stop never binds a bound step again), not the CXC oracle (CXC v0.2.40, commit 3c1459ac, has no session engine). The Go side is internal/flow/engine/session.go: sessionStop and sessionStopBinds, which the stop reaches from the send_input bind (sessionBindStopped) and from the adoption of a recorded subagent (sessionAdopt). The tests are TestSessionStopBindsAStepOnceWhenItsReadHoldsTwoTurnsOfIt and TestSessionAdoptStopBindsTheAdoptedStepOnce in internal/flow/engine/session_1409_test.go.

- No test passed through the stop loop where the loop's own verify binds the step first and a second running turn that carries the same marker follows, so the rule that a bound step keeps its turn (sessionStopBinds) was not pinned on that path. The two tests now pin it: the send_input bind's stop over the memory and SQLite journals, and the adoption's stop over the memory journal. Each shows the second turn neither bound again nor interrupted, and removing the guard in sessionStopBinds makes both fail with a second turn_started row for the step. The adoption is pinned over memory only: over SQLite, a death at thread_started loses the bind's rows with its batch, so the resumed call does not adopt. port: fixed (pinned by the two tests; there is no product change).

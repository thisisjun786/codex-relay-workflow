# CRW-1098 — provider status probe held past its 8 second budget

Source: `plugins/codexclaw/components/provider-bridge/src/cli.ts:59-62` and `detect.ts:54` at v0.2.40 (commit 3c1459ac), and `bin/codexclaw.mjs:256-260,606-608`; the Go side is `internal/provider/cli.go` (`readStatus`, `Run`, `Help`) and `detect.go`. Found by the 10-10 CXC improvement review (A8-09, P2) and the isolated real-use trial (the probe blocked for 10.01 s where the oracle's `spawnSync` returned at 8.04 s).

- The Go probe bounded the process with `CommandContext` but wired stdout and stderr to `io.Writer`s with no `WaitDelay`, so `cmd.Run` also waited for the copy goroutines: when ocx printed its payload and exited while a child it had started still held the pipes, the probe waited for that child, past the 8 second budget and up to the SessionStart hook's 20 second limit (source: the oracle's `spawnSync` timeout ends the wait whatever holds the pipes; this defect is the port's own); port: fixed (running ocx and draining its output share the one 8 second budget, and `cmd.WaitDelay` of 500 ms closes the probe's own ends of the pipes after ocx has ended or been stopped, so the probe returns within the budget plus that grace. Only ocx itself is killed at the budget; a child of ocx is not the probe's and is never killed to close a pipe. A complete payload with exit 0 is the answer even if a child still holds the pipes; a partial one is an error line saying the pipe was left open after a partial payload. Pinned by `TestStatusPipeHeldByAChildEndsWithinTheBudget`, `TestStatusPartialJSONFromAnExitedOcxWhoseChildHoldsThePipe`).
- A probe that ran out of budget, printed more than 1 MiB or was stopped all came out as `ocx status exited null`, which does not say which; port: fixed (the error line says `ocx status timed out after 8s` or `ocx status output exceeded 1048576 bytes`, a partial payload followed by a hang is the timeout, and a cancelled caller ends the command with 130 and no line. Detect-only is unchanged: no provider is activated or started, SessionStart stays non-blocking, and the `native`, `provider` and `error` modes keep their shape. Pinned by `TestStatusNeverFinishingOcxIsAnErrorWithATimeoutDiagnosis`, `TestStatusPartialJSONThenHangIsAnError`, `TestStatusOutputOverTheLimitIsAnError`, `TestStatusCancellationEndsTheProbeWithoutALine`, `TestStatusFastAnswerDoesNotWaitForTheGrace`, `TestStatusTimeoutAndBufferLimit`).
- Supersedes known-defects.md, section "Found by the CRW-344 provider detection and map launcher port", item beginning "The provider dispatcher ignores every argument": now port: fixed (`crw provider --help`, `-h` and `help` print the command's help and exit 0 without looking for ocx; an unknown first word exits 2 with the usage on stderr, also without a probe; `detect` and `status` probe, and words after them are still ignored, because the CXC name table puts `detect` before whatever followed `provider`, which keeps the recorded `provider --help` fixtures as they were. Pinned by `TestHelpAndUnknownVerbsAnswerBeforeTheProbe` and `TestProviderMapCommandIngress`).
- Supersedes known-defects.md, section "Found by the CRW-344 provider detection and map launcher port", item beginning "Any numeric listen.port is accepted": now port: fixed (a `listen.port` that is a number must be a whole number from 1 to 65535, anything else (0, a negative, a fraction, above 65535, one that overflows to infinity, `-0`) turns the line into `mode: error` with `ocx status reported a listen.port that is not a TCP port: <as ocx wrote it>`; a port that is absent, null or not a number is still no port (null), as in the oracle. Pinned by `TestListenPortMustBeAValidTCPPort`; the three recorded inputs `port: 1e400`, `-0` and `1.5` of `testdata/oracle.jsonl` are the changed cases in `recordedPortChanges`).

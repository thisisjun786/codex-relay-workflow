# CRW-1116 — the reviewer's GO-WITH-FIXES (blockers=N) verdict

Source: CXC v0.2.40 (commit 3c1459ac) `plugins/codexclaw/components/pabcd-state/src/review-round.ts:372-390` (`parseReviewSignoff`), `review-observer.ts:57-164` (the SubagentStop observer that records it) and `review-round-cli.ts:192-208` (`renderOpenPacket`). The Go files are `internal/pabcd/review/signoff.go` (`ParseSignoff`) and `internal/pabcd/review/verdictline.go` (the verdict-line grammar), `internal/pabcd/hook/reviewobserver.go` (`HandleReviewObserver`), `internal/pabcd/goalplan/types.go` and `revive.go` (the lane's `blockers` and `findings`), `internal/pabcd/cli/orchestrate_review_binding.go` (the A>B check), `internal/pabcd/cli/review_round_args.go` (the open packet) and `internal/relay/delivery/report_revision.go` (the relay's verdict line). The skill side is `plugins/crw/skills/crw-dev-code-reviewer/SKILL.md` (REVIEW-OUTPUT-01) and `plugins/crw/skills/crw-pabcd/references/phase-audit.md`.

- The sign-off parser accepts only the bare words PASS, FAIL, NEAR-PASS and GO-WITH-FIXES, while REVIEW-OUTPUT-01 (the reviewer skill) and the relay's report renderer (`internal/relay/delivery/report_revision.go`, I-93) both write `VERDICT: GO-WITH-FIXES (blockers=N)`; a reviewer that followed the instructions was read as no sign-off, the round stayed in flight and `review_signoff_unparsed` was noted, while the bare word was recorded as a near-pass (`review-round.ts:372`); port: fixed (one grammar, `review.parseVerdictLine`, reads the bare words and `GO-WITH-FIXES|NEAR-PASS (blockers=N[; findings=a,b])`, N a whole number from 1 to 9999 and the references of `goalplan.ValidFindingRef`; the verdict is recorded as the same near-pass with the count and references kept on the round's lane (`blockers`, `findings`), never as a near-pass with the suffix cut off. A zero, negative, signed, non-numeric, empty or over-large count, a count on PASS or FAIL, an unknown parenthesis and any text after it are no verdict, as before; the relay renders its line through `review.BlockerSuffix` and `review.MaxBlockers`, so the renderer and the parser cannot drift. The bindings are unchanged: a wrong launch, another reviewer, a re-planned epoch or a moved work-phase still ignore the sign-off. A plan file whose content changed after the round opened is not the observer's check: the observer records the verdict it was given, and the A>B edge refuses it because the recomputed plan hash differs ("the plan changed after round"), while the same attest over an unchanged plan enters B (`TestReviewObserverDecoratedVerdictOverAChangedPlanFileIsRefusedAtAToB`). Pinned by `TestParseSignoffReadsTheBlockerCount`, `TestVerdictLineRenderingAndParsingAgree`, `TestRecordVerdictKeepsTheBlockerCountAndFindings`, `TestReviveLaneKeepsBlockersAndFindings`, `TestReviewObserverRecordsTheBlockerCountOfAGoWithFixesVerdict`, `TestReviewObserverDecoratedVerdictKeepsTheBindings` and `TestRenderedVerdictLineIsReadByTheSignoffParser`).
- The oracle's open packet tells the reviewer to close with `VERDICT: PASS | NEAR-PASS | FAIL`, so a reviewer that followed the packet and one that followed the skill wrote different lines; port: fixed (the packet names `VERDICT: PASS | NEAR-PASS | FAIL | GO-WITH-FIXES (blockers=N)`; the oracle replays of the packet apply the same one-line rename, as they do the R9 role header, in `review_round_args_test.go` and `review_round_run_test.go`. The contract fixtures that carry the packet are still pending and are not replayed).
- The A>B check never saw a blocker count: a recorded near-pass needed the attest's `auditResidual` to be nonempty and nothing more (`orchestrate-cli.ts:49-95`, `attest.ts`); port: fixed (a round whose recorded verdict is a near-pass with a count N above zero is a required review with unresolved blockers, and `orchestrateReviewBindingCheckPlan` refuses the edge unless the attest carries `auditBlockers`: exactly one `{"blocker": n, "disposition": "folded" | "rebutted", "reason": "..."}` for each blocker 1..N, n a whole number, the disposition one of the two fixed tokens and the reason nonempty (`attest.Attestation.DisposesBlockers`). The check is on that structure only and never reads the prose of `auditResidual`: a first version searched the residual for the substrings "fold" or "rebut", which passed "blocker 1 not folded", "unfolded blockers remain" and "manifold risks remain" and refused a correct residual written in Korean or with other words. `auditResidual` stays the main agent's own words in any language, and whether a reason is sound is its judgment (AUDIT-LOOP-01). The near-pass itself stays allowed, so the main agent still folds or rebuts the audit's blockers; a round with no recorded verdict (an optional review that never ran or never parsed), a legacy bare verdict with no count, a FAIL (handled as before) and the attest, audit-output and near-pass-residual checks of `internal/pabcd/attest/attest.go` are unchanged, and no in-flight round is blocked (LEAN-REVIEW-01). Pinned by `TestOrchestrateReviewBlockersNeedAnExplicitDisposition` (the recorded counterexamples, Korean and English residuals with structured dispositions, and each malformed list), `TestOrchestrateReviewBlockersOptionalReviewDoesNotBlock` and `TestCoerceCarriesAuditBlockersAndDisposesThem`).
- The Stop block's A>B command example carried only `auditResidual` for the near-pass, so an agent that filled the shown placeholders after a counted verdict was refused for the missing `auditBlockers` (post-evaluation); port: fixed (`stopNextCommands[A]` and the win32 `--attest-file` form carry `"auditBlockers":"<only when the review recorded blockers=N: replace with one {blocker,disposition,reason} object per blocker, disposition folded|rebutted>"`, a placeholder that is ignored when the verdict has no count; pinned by `TestStopBlockTextOfEveryPhase`; the recorded fixture `hook__stop-checking-pabcd-continuation__stop_budget_three_per_phase` is claimed as intentionally changed in `contract/notes/cxc/CRW-1116.json`).

# CRW-1366 — an authority question was asked only of an approved DESIGN, again on every re-plan, and a reuse hid its route

Source: CRW's own design, not the CXC oracle (CXC v0.2.40, commit 3c1459ac): the oracle has no flow engine, no architect, no explorer routing and no design audit. The Go and script side is `internal/flow/ops/lane_decisions.go` (`crw.exploreRoute`, `crw.exploreRecord`, `askedDigests`, `orchestratorAnswer`), `internal/flow/workflows/project-lane.js` (`auditedDesign`, `askOrchestrator`), `internal/flow/workflows/lib/lane.js` (`explore`) and `internal/flow/workflows/lib/packets.js` (`routeLine` of the design audit packet). Found by the pre-merge evaluation and the final verification of CRW-1347 (/state/crw/crw-lane-open-questions-1347/opusverify-final-abd42a3f.json).

- Supersedes CRW-1347.md, item beginning "Named limit: the ask of the orchestrator is raised when the DESIGN is approved": now port: fixed (an open question with an `authority:*` scope is asked of the orchestrator in the non-blocking `design_question` escalation of the audit round that routed it, before that round's audit, so a DESIGN that is never approved asks it before the `design_audit_rounds` escalation; the lane goes on. The escalation's `evidence.questions` names the question digests it asked, and the router reads those rows, never rewriting one: a later round or a re-plan that raises the same question routes it `asked_in_run` and raises no ask of its own, and once a `decision_applied` row answers the ask, the question reuses that answer (`answered_by_orchestrator`) from the next round on. The questions beyond the explorer cap are still asked when the DESIGN is approved. Pinned by `TestAuthorityQuestionIsAskedWhenTheAuditRoundsRunOut`, `TestOrchestratorAnswerIsUsedOnTheNextAuditRound`, `TestReplanAsksTheSameAuthorityQuestionOnce`, `TestExploreRoutingRemembersOrchestratorAsks` and `TestOpenQuestionRoutesThroughDesignAudit`).
- A question repeated in its round after the first went to the orchestrator showed only "reused" in the design audit packet, and a reuse of an answer from an earlier round did not say where the answer came from (`crw.exploreRoute`, `routeLine` in `packets.js`); port: fixed (a reuse records the route its answer came from in the optional field `origin` of the route, the explore row, the answers of `crw.exploreRecord` and the decision row's inputs - `spawn` for an explorer, `orchestrator` - and the packet shows `(original route: explorer)` or `(original route: orchestrator)`; a row written before this has no `origin` and shows none. Pinned by `TestOrchestratorAnswerIsUsedOnTheNextAuditRound` and `TestExploreRoutingRemembersOrchestratorAsks`).
- An explorer's answer to a question with no notation is reused when a later round raises the same question with an `authority:` notation, so that question does not go to the orchestrator (the order of the cases in `crw.exploreRoute`); port: kept (reuse first is the decided rule of CRW-1347 c1; the reuse now shows `(original route: explorer)`).
- `crw flow answer` answers only the latest `design_question` escalation of a run (all of them are the asks `design` of no node, and an earlier one is a replaced revision, `Questions` in `internal/flow/cli/runs.go`), and the digest lists only that one as waiting. With an ask in each round and a question asked once, an earlier ask was replaced by the next while its question was asked nowhere else: a question of round 1 that round 2 raised again beside a new authority question, or the authority question of the round the approval's explorer-cap ask followed, could no longer be answered or seen as waiting (found by the pre-merge evaluation of 7d4302c9, d1); port: fixed (each ask carries, besides the questions it raises, the questions of its DESIGN that the run asked already and no answer has reached - routed `asked_in_run`, and at the approval the round's `out_of_authority` ones - and names their digests in its evidence, so the current ask holds every question still waiting. Pinned through `flowcli.Questions` and `flowcli.Judge` by `TestANewAskCarriesTheAuthorityQuestionsStillUnanswered` and `TestTheExplorerCapAskCarriesTheAuthorityQuestionOfItsRound`).
- Named limit: a question the run asked that a later DESIGN no longer raises is carried by no later ask, so once a later ask replaces its escalation it is no longer answerable; port: kept (the DESIGN that raised it was revised without it, and an unanswered ask stops nothing).
- Named limit: the orchestrator answers an ask as a whole, so a question reuses the text of the answer to the ask that carried it (`orchestratorAnswer`), and an ask of several questions gives each of them the whole answer, the other questions' part with it; port: kept (one answer text for each ask is the shape of the `decision_applied` row; the packet shows the answer as given).
- Named limit: the answers `crw.explore` hands `crw.exploreRecord` carry the new `origin` and the design audit packet's route lines changed, and an authority question is asked in its audit round, so a run that recorded its open questions under the earlier binary runs `crw.exploreRecord` live again on a resume and, where the packet or the asks differ, the ask and `design-audit:r1`, and a run past its design audit comes to the refusal of `crw.designLedger` that CRW-1321.md and CRW-1347.md describe (the header of `crw:lib/packets`); port: kept (a resumed lane builds the packet it built).

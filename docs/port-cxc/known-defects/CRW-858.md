# CRW-858 — spawn hook: the depth return preceded the final-gate prerequisite check

Source: CXC v0.2.40 (commit 3c1459ac) `plugins/codexclaw/components/subagent-config/src/spawn-attach-hook.ts:1074-1079`
(`checkFinalGatePrereqs` -> `denyEnvelope`) runs before the `JSON.stringify` at `:1103` whose `RangeError` a
`tool_input` nested too deep causes, caught at `:1111` into nothing. Go port: `internal/role/spawn/hook_route.go`
`spawnHookRoute` against `CheckFinalGatePrereqs` (`internal/role/spawn/finalgate.go`).

## Defects

- The port's `spawnHookRoute` returned nothing as soon as `spawnHookRouteDeep(a.toolInput)` was true, before it ran
  `CheckFinalGatePrereqs`, so a spawn packet that marked itself as the final gate and nested an unrelated field deep
  was allowed where the oracle denies it (the oracle's check at `:1074-1079` precedes the `JSON.stringify` at
  `:1103`); port: fixed (this issue computes the depth judgement first, reads the items shallowly and joins their text
  (or the message) for the gate, runs the gate, and only then applies the depth return, so a deep packet the gate refuses
  is denied — pinned by `TestSpawnHookDeepGateRefusesBeforeTheDepthReturn` and
  `TestSpawnHookDeepGateItemsMarkerInALaterItem`).
- The oracle fails open for one further shape where this port fails closed: an items-form packet with a field nested deep
  *inside* an item makes the oracle's own `JSON.stringify(updatedItems)` at `:1068` throw, which its outer catch at
  `:1111` turns into nothing before the gate at `:1074` is reached, while this port skips only the item/input
  comparison that recurses and still runs the gate on the joined item text
  (`internal/role/spawn/hook_route.go` `spawnHookRoute`); port: fixed (the issue's answer is the gate first and fail
  closed, and the recorded corpus has no such case; the security direction the parity rule revision of 2026-10-03 keeps
  — pinned by `TestSpawnHookDeepGateItemsMarkerInALaterItem` and
  `TestSpawnHookDeepGateItemsWithoutMarkerPrintsNothing`).

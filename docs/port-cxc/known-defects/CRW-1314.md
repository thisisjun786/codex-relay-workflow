# CRW-1314 — a project's upward messages could not reach the management session that bound its owner

Source: CRW's own design (CRW-1314 §1, §2 and §5, lane D1c; two session layers, management and project owner, Jun 10-14), not the CXC oracle (CXC v0.2.40, commit 3c1459ac): the oracle walks a project's report up to the initiative supervisor and, without one, folds in the store seat; it has no management level. The Go side is `internal/relay/registry/linkage_write.go` (`bindScopeKind`, `managementEdgePlan`, `applyManagementEdge`), `internal/relay/supervisor/hierarchy.go` (`withStoreSeat`, `resolveLevels`), `internal/relay/supervisor/direct.go` (`directLayer`) and `internal/flow/report` (`recipient.go` `StoreResolver`, `Deliverable`; `report.go` `message`). Found by the 10-13 field trial (P-CRW-140; CRW-1315 40, D8, L7) as restated for two layers: with the initiative lead off the 1.0 path, a project's report fell through to the single store seat and never reached the per-thread management seat (CRW-1333) of the session that runs the project.

- Binding a project owner recorded nothing about the management session that did it; port: fixed (`linkage-bind --role parent --scope <PROJECT> --creator <thread>`, default the caller's `CODEX_THREAD_ID`, writes the execution edge management -> project in the binding's transaction when the creator holds a live management seat, answered under `managementLink`; a second session's binding is refused `link_conflict` and commits only its conflict row, another owner is refused `duplicate_scope_owner` as before, and the seat's handover moves the edge's `upper_task_id` with `scope_handover`). Pinned by `TestProjectOwnerBindingWritesTheManagementEdge`, `TestProjectOwnerBindingSecondManagementTakesOnlyByHandover`.
- A project's upward messages were addressed to the initiative supervisor or the store seat only; port: fixed (`resolveLevels` takes the owner of a management level directly above the project as the recipient, `recipient.scopeKind` `management`, and `withStoreSeat` does not fold the store seat over it; the order is the management seat, the legacy lead, the store seat; a seat with no live owner is refused `unregistered_scope`, competing parents stay refused; the path is the same for a relationship's report, a `project:<key>` notice and the direct project kinds, whose envelope names the `management` layer and `orchestrator_to_management`, or the `lead` layer under a legacy lead). Pinned by `TestProjectResolutionReachesTheManagementSeat`, `TestProjectResolutionManagementSeatWithoutOwnerWaits`, `TestProjectResolutionTwoParentsIsRefused`, `TestProjectResolutionKeepsTheLegacyOrder`, `TestDirectStageProjectReachesTheManagementSeat`, and end to end over the scripted App Server by `TestManagementProjectE2ETwoSessionsEachHearOnlyTheirOwnProject`, `TestManagementProjectE2EHandoverMovesTheLink`, `TestManagementProjectE2EStoreSeatFallback`.
- `crw flow report-up` named only an initiative lead as a deliverable recipient, and a `jun_decision_request` always the store seat; port: fixed (`StoreResolver` answers `recipient_state` `management` with the seat owner's thread, delivered, for every report kind of a project under a management seat, `jun_decision_request` included; the legacy lead next; the store seat last, now recorded as `store` with its thread and still not sent through the bridge; a `jun_decision_request` with no management seat stays the store seat's; the notice to a management session names `crw flow reports --project P --unread`, a listing of that project's reports alone, so two management sessions do not read each other's; any other notice with no initiative names `crw flow reports --unread`). Pinned by `TestReportUpReachesTheManagementSeat`, `TestJunDecisionReportReachesTheManagementSeat`, `TestReportRecipientsOfTwoManagementSessions`, `TestReportUpStoreSeatFoldStaysStanding`, `TestJunDecisionReportIsARowForTheStoreSeat`, `TestReportsOfOneProjectForItsManagementSession`, `TestReportsListsTheReportsOfOneProject`.
- Deviation: a project an initiative lead already supervises (the legacy configuration) keeps that edge when a management session binds its owner: the binding is not refused, no management edge is written, the answer carries `managementLinkSkipped` and one `management_link_skipped` journal row records it (`TestProjectOwnerBindingUnderALegacyInitiativeKeepsIt`); a store-seat fold of a flow report is recorded as the store seat's (`recipient_state` `store`) rather than `unregistered_scope`, and is still not delivered; a `jun_decision_request` skips a legacy lead (design §5: a lead does not decide for Jun); the relay store's schema is unchanged (new values in `scope_links.upper_kind` and `journal.kind` only, no CHECK; `relay_schema_shipped.json` and its compat test untouched); the transport of flow reports over the supervisor channel (`RelaySender`), `jun_decision_request` through `project:<key>` on that channel, decision authority over several seats and the skill procedures are lanes D2, D3 and D4; port: kept (reversible code and row values only, Jun 10-13: warn and record, fail open).

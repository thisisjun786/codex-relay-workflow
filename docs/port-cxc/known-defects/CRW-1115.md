# CRW-1115 — the SubagentStop evidence gate looked only at the parent's native cwd

Source: CXC v0.2.40 (commit 3c1459ac) `plugins/codexclaw/components/pabcd-state/src/subagent-evidence.ts:449` and `:489` (the receipt is judged under the payload cwd's evidence directory only) and `plugins/codexclaw/components/subagent-config/src/spawn-attach-hook.ts:972-987` (the guard the child's packet gets). The Go side is `internal/pabcd/hook/subagentstop.go` (the gate), `internal/pabcd/evidence/assignment.go` (the assignment record and its checks), `internal/pabcd/evidence/lock.go`, `internal/role/spawn/guard.go` (`EvidenceAssignmentBlock`, the packet lines) and `internal/role/spawn/hook.go` / `hook_route.go` (the injection and the record). Found by the 10-10 combined review, B1-01.

- A subagent's SubagentStop payload carries its parent's native cwd, and the oracle accepts a receipt only under that cwd's `.crw/evidence`. A bounded worker that its parent assigned a separate managed worktree (dispatch-surfaces: the packet requires that path as every command's workdir) records its receipt in that worktree and is blocked at attempt 1, while identical bytes in the parent's tree pass; a packet whose scope allows no evidence write at all can only spend its three retries and leave an unresolved verdict (subagent-evidence.ts:449, :489); port: fixed. The parent's packet now declares the contract on its own lines, `CRW-WORKTREE: <absolute path>` and, for a packet that allows no evidence write, `CRW-EVIDENCE: none`. The spawn hook (the parent's own tool call, the only trusted point) records an evidence assignment for the parent's exact session under `.crw/evidence-assignments/<session>-<digest>/<id>.json`, once the spawn is allowed, and injects `[CRW-EVIDENCE-ASSIGNMENT:<id>]` after the guard with the approved receipt directory, or with the scope-conflict line. An ambiguous request (two trees, a relative or missing tree, another evidence value) is refused with a deny envelope rather than guessed. The gate accepts a receipt of a registered tree assignment only when the tree is still the registered directory (real path, device and inode; a link swapped in or a recreated directory is refused), its `.crw` and `.crw/evidence` are real directories, the receipt passes the native checks inside that root, it is not older than the dispatch, and the tree's HEAD at dispatch is still an ancestor of its HEAD (an unrelated history is foreign). The first agent whose receipt passes claims the assignment; another agent's receipt in the same tree is refused. A child never names a root itself. A no-write packet ends with `EVIDENCE_SCOPE_CONFLICT: <id>`: the child is released at once with a resolvable unverified verdict (no retry spent, nothing written in its tree), the assignment is marked scope-conflict, and completion stays denied until the parent records its own verification and runs `crw pabcd evidence resolve`; an unknown or borrowed id is not an exit. Without the packet lines nothing is recorded and the native cwd stays the only root. Explorers stay outside the gate and no relay or dispatch-ledger registration is made. Pinned by `internal/role/spawn/evidence_assignment_test.go` (`TestEvidenceAssignmentTreeReceiptPasses`, `TestEvidenceAssignmentAbsentKeepsNativeRoot`, `TestEvidenceAssignmentRefusals`, `TestEvidenceAssignmentScopeConflict`, `TestEvidenceAssignmentNativeHelperStaysUnregistered`, `TestEvidenceAssignmentRequestRefusalsAndIdempotence`). The skill text that tells a coordinator to write these lines (dispatch-surfaces.md, delegation.md) is CRW-1115's follow-up in the skills lane (N13).

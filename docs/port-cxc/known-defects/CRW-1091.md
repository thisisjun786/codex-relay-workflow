# CRW-1091 — one metrics reading per Stop, and nothing after the announced cap

Source: CXC v0.2.40 (commit 3c1459ac) `plugins/codexclaw/components/pabcd-state/src/hook.ts` `observeProgress` (:1465-1490), `bumpStopCounter` (:1497-1521), `handleStop` (:1805-1873) and `metrics.ts` `readObjectiveMetrics` (:69), `readObjectiveKind`, `checkObjectivePlateau` (:207). The Go side is `internal/pabcd/hook/stop.go` (`stopHandle`, `stopCounted`, `stopSnapshot`, `stopBump`, `stopObserveProgress`, `stopObjectivePlateau`, `stopReadWorkContext`, `stopGoalIdleBlock`) with the pure helpers of `internal/pabcd/metric/metrics.go` (`JudgeNewRows`, `PlateauOf`, `InferObjectiveKind`, CRW-1088). Found by the 10-10 CXC improvement review (A3-04).

- One Stop of an active goal mid-cycle reads and parses the whole shared metrics ledger up to four times (the cursor, the progress plateau, the objective-kind inference without a kind file, the emitted plateau; measured 10,000 rows/1.74 MB median 162.25 ms and 50,000 rows/8.7 MB 768.32 ms), and the bound goalplan once for every question asked of it (hook.ts:1470,1855, metrics.ts:69); port: fixed (inside the session lock, after the goal, the turn and the binding are judged again on what the lock found, the Stop reads the ledger once and keeps the bound goalplan the in-lock check validated (`stopSnapshot`); the cursor, the progress judgment, the kind inference, the plateau, the work phase and the block text are computed from that one reading. Nothing read before the lock stands in for it, so a pause, an approval wait or a new turn landing before the lock still releases (`TestStopReleasesWhenTheWorldMovedBeforeTheLock`), and nothing is kept between events. The approval-wait check before the lock still reads the plan once, and the render advisory keeps its own reads at C. Pinned by `TestCRW1091OneStopReadsTheLedgerOnce`, which counts the kernel's open events (inotify) on the ledger and the plan: one ledger open and two plan opens, against two and five at the CRW-1088 head (the oracle shape read the ledger four times). Red output: `/state/crw/crw-lane-pabcd-stop/evidence/red-1091.log`).
- After the turn's total cap was announced (`stopBlockCapNotified`), every further Stop of the turn still reads the ledger and the plan, advances the total (25 to 26 and on) and the cursor, and rewrites the state, only to answer nothing (hook.ts:1497-1521); port: fixed (a Stop whose state shows the announced cap returns before the plan or the ledger is read and writes nothing, judged on the first reading and again under the lock, where the turn stamp joins the binding comparison, so a cap that another Stop of the same turn announced while this one waited for the lock is not announced twice. The first announcement is still written with the counter before it is answered, once per turn; UserPromptSubmit's turn stamp clears it. An interactive session's render advisory is answered before this check, as before. Pinned by `TestCRW1091AnnouncedTotalCapReadsAndWritesNothing` (no ledger or plan open and no create, rename or write in the sessions directory over three capped Stops, with new rows recorded between them) and `TestCRW1091ACapAnnouncedBeforeTheLockIsNotAnnouncedAgain` (removing the check under the lock turns it red: `/state/crw/crw-lane-pabcd-stop/evidence/mutation-1091-inlock-cap-removed.log`); `TestStopTotalCapEndsTheLoopAndIsAnnouncedOnce`, the harness `TestStopLegRepeatedEventIsBounded` and the recorded fixture `hook__stop-checking-pabcd-continuation__total_cap_releases_with_system_message` keep the single announcement).
- Supersedes CRW-192.md, item beginning "After a per-phase release the counter is reset": the clause "the total keeps advancing on every Stop after the cap" is now port: fixed (as the item above; the per-phase part is CRW-1086.md).
- The continuation, goal-idle, plateau and render advisory texts are unchanged by this restructuring (`TestStopBlockTextOfEveryPhase`, `TestStopGoalIdleBlockNamesTheWork`, `TestStopPlateauBlock`, `TestStopPlateauBlockKeepsTheObservationAdvisories`, `TestStopRenderAdvisory` and the recorded Stop fixtures replay byte for byte); the work context of a block is built from the plan the in-lock check read through the slug check of `safeReadBoundGoalplan` (hook.ts:1430-1436), where the oracle's `readStopWorkContext` read it again without that check; every slug `goalplan.ValidateGoalplanSlug` accepts passes that check, so the two readings name the same plan.

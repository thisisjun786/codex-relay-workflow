# CRW-1403 — follow-up of CRW-1379: an indented line that is not a bullet cleared the state of the bullet above it in the plan input

Source: CRW's own design, not the CXC oracle (CXC v0.2.40, commit 3c1459ac, has no flow compile and no tracking milestone). The Go file is `internal/flow/planinput/body.go` (`bullets`); the tests are in `internal/flow/planinput/planinput_test.go`. No relay store schema, dag rule, refusal code, receipt status or skill is added or changed, and the reading of a body without an indented non-bullet line is unchanged.

- An indented line that was not a bullet and followed a `Blocked by:` bullet, or a sub-bullet that was left out with one, met no item to extend and cleared the parent and the dropped state; so the sub-bullets after it became end conditions of their own, and the sub-bullets of a `Blocked by:` item after it were kept as end conditions (CRW-1379 handoff, open item "indented Blocked by"; Argon V5 P2 of CRW-1379); port: fixed (an indented non-bullet line leaves the state alone: it extends the item above it, or is left out with a `Blocked by:` item, and the sub-bullets after it are read as they were before it, so a later sub-bullet of a criterion joins the criterion and a later sub-bullet of a `Blocked by:` item is left out. A blank line before the line still ends the continuation, as before. Pinned by `TestIndentedContinuationKeepsTheStateOfItsBullet`).
- An indented plain `Blocked by:` line (no bullet marker) after a criterion was joined to the criterion's text, and one with no item above it left its own sub-bullets as end conditions (CRW-1379 handoff, open item; `tracking.ParseBlockedBy` reads the line at any indent, bullet or not); port: fixed (the line is an order and not a part of any item, the item above it stays the parent of the sub-bullets after it, and the bullets indented deeper than the line are left out with it, as for a `Blocked by:` bullet. A line at the indent of the item's sub-bullets leaves a later sub-bullet at the same indent in the item, and a line with no item above it leaves a list at its own indent flat. The same reading holds for the lists of edit regions, where the line is not a path. A `Blocked by:` line that is not indented ends the list, as before. Pinned by `TestIndentedContinuationKeepsTheStateOfItsBullet`).

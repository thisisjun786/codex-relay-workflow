# CRW-632 — the metric record, metric kind and divergence writers end their lock waits with the interrupt

Source: `plugins/codexclaw/components/pabcd-state/src/metric-cli.ts` (the `record` branch :103-116 and the `kind` branch :136-144), `metrics.ts` (`recordObjectiveMetric` :119-137 and `writeObjectiveKind` :138-153) and `divergence.ts` (`writeDivergenceMode` :112-140 and `recordDivergenceCandidate` :169-200) at v0.2.40 (commit 3c1459ac), through the ports in `internal/pabcd/metric/metrics.go`, `internal/pabcd/metric/divergence.go`, `internal/pabcd/cli/metric.go`, `internal/pabcd/cli/divergence.go` and `internal/harness/pabcd_cli.go`.

- The oracle has no lock and no signal handler, so its process dies at the first interrupt and writes nothing more (the sources above); the port's `metric record`, `metric kind`, `divergence mode` and `divergence candidate add` asked for their exclusive lock in the kernel with no deadline, so a cancelled run waited for the holder and then wrote (port-introduced, not an upstream defect: the locks are this port's own; in the red run of `TestPabcdWritersEndOnTheFirstInterruptWhileTheLockIsHeld` the built crw held at each of the four locks was still running 5 s after the SIGINT); port: fixed (CRW-632: the four writers take the invocation's context through `RecordObjectiveMetricContext`, `WriteObjectiveKindContext`, `WriteDivergenceModeContext` and `RecordDivergenceCandidateContext`, ask for the lock with `metricLockWait` (LOCK_EX|LOCK_NB, again every 20 ms) and read `ctx.Err()` once more after the lock is taken, whether or not the wait had to wait, before writing; the plain forms and a `context.Background` caller keep the blocking wait, so every uninterrupted run and every other caller is unchanged).
- Supersedes known-defects.md, section "Found by the metric ingest record-window port (CRW-627)", item beginning "The other writers of these files still wait for their locks without a deadline": now port: fixed (CRW-632 covers all four writers the line named: `metric record` and `metric kind` in `metrics.go`, and the mode-directory lock of `WriteDivergenceMode` and the archive lock of `appendCandidateRow` in `divergence.go`; each ends its wait with the invocation's context, closes without writing and returns the context's own error, and the CLI entry points `RunMetricCLIContext` and the new `RunDivergenceCliContext` hand that error to the harness rows, which answer Interrupted (130) with nothing printed. The behaviour is otherwise unchanged, so no corpus fixture's expectation moves).

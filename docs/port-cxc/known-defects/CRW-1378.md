# CRW-1378 — a repeated question under another management seat folded into the first seat's record, and three smaller gaps of the decision authority

Source: CRW's own design (CRW-1314 §5, the CRW-1371 lane's evaluation), not the CXC oracle (CXC v0.2.40, commit 3c1459ac): the oracle has no management seat. The Go side is `internal/relay/store/userdecisions.go` (`RaiseWhere`), `internal/relay/cli/decisions.go` (`runDecisionRaise`, `decisionSeat`, `decisionManagementAuthority`) and `internal/relay/argparse/specs.json`.

- The fingerprint of a question does not carry its authority, so the same question raised from a project under another management seat folded into the open record the first seat held (`decisions.Merge` keeps the first record), and the second seat's owner was refused `authority_unverified` with no warning; port: fixed (`store.RaiseWhere` folds only into an open or raised record its predicate accepts, oldest first; `decision-raise` accepts a record held under the same seat - the same `authority.ref` of a user-grade or delegated-management-grade record, or none - and otherwise inserts a record of its own, answered under `foldWarning` (`seat_differs`, the records it did not fold into) and journaled as `decision_authority_warning`; the fingerprint and `Merge` are unchanged, so old records read and answer as before and a question under one seat, or under the store seat, folds as it did). Pinned by `TestDecisionRaiseUnderAnotherSeatIsANewRecord` with the existing CRW-1371 and CRW-737 decision tests.
- With no management edge above the project, a ref the raiser gave that names a known management seat stayed with no warning, so that seat's owner alone could answer and the store seat could not; port: fixed (the ref is kept, warned under `authorityWarning` with `reason` `ref_without_edge` and journaled once; never refused). Pinned by `TestDecisionRaiseRefWithoutAnEdgeWarns`.
- A record whose seat has no live owner can be answered by nobody, and `docs/relay/linkage.md` did not say how to recover; port: fixed (the recovery - bind the seat's key again, hand the seat over, or withdraw and raise again - is written in linkage.md; characterized by `TestDecisionOfAnOwnerlessSeatIsRecoveredAsLinkageSays`, no behaviour change).
- `decision-answer --authority`'s help still said the answer's `--by` must be the store-scope supervisor binding's task; port: fixed (it names the owner of the management seat the record's ref names, else the store seat's task). Pinned by `TestDecisionAnswerAuthorityHelpNamesTheManagementSeat`.
- Deviation: a seat is compared as the record's `authority.ref` of the two grades; a parent-class record has none, so a parent-class raise and a raise of the same question under a management seat do not fold either; the relay store's schema is unchanged (no table, column or index; the fingerprint index is not unique); the only refusal is the existing `authority_unverified` identity check; port: kept (warn and record, fail open).

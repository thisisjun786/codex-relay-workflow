# CRW-1390 — the pump told a decision for Jun the supervisor channel delivered, an uncertain flow send left no record, and the D2 and c1 follow-ups

Source: CRW's own design (CRW-1314 §6, lane D4, with the follow-ups of lanes D2 (CRW-1380, CRW-1372, CRW-1378) and c1 (CRW-1349, CRW-1361)), not the CXC oracle (CXC v0.2.40, commit 3c1459ac): the oracle has no flow run, no management seat and no direct message on the supervisor channel. The Go side is `internal/manage` (`pump_flow_sources.go` `pumpChannelHolds`, `config.go`, `pump.go`), `internal/relay/supervisor` (`flow_delivery.go`, `recover.go`, `readback.go`), `internal/flow/report` (`relay.go`, `hook.go`, `send.go`), `internal/relay/cli` (`decisions.go`, `reportup.go`), `internal/relay/capacity` (tests only) and `internal/flow/cli` (`next.go`, `session.go`, `lock.go`, `process.go`, `resume.go`).

Pinned by `TestPumpLeavesAJunDecisionTheSupervisorChannelHoldsOrDelivered`, with `TestJunDecisionRoutesToManagerPump` (no delivery row) unchanged.
- `management_thread` and the pump's management batch did not say that the supervisor channel is the 1.0 path between the parent and its children (CRW-1314 §0: kept for reading until the relay path is confirmed in real use) (manage/config.go `Config.ManagementThread`, manage/pump.go); port: kept (marked superseded in the code and in `docs/flow/reporting.md`, "The pump on the 1.0 path"; the configuration key, its checks and the batch are unchanged, so a configuration without a management seat keeps working).
- A flow message whose send had an unknown outcome (`held_uncertain`, by the settle or by the recovery of a send stranded after its transport started) wrote no delivery row, so its report read `queued` for ever and only the pump's `report_unread` showed it (D2 follow-up; independent verification of 4d293b7b) (supervisor/flow_delivery.go `flowDeliveryResult`, supervisor/recover.go `recoverStranded`); port: fixed (the same transaction appends `report_delivery`/`message_delivery` `unknown`; the verified readback that reconciles the send appends `accepted` (supervisor/readback.go); a replay of the report that finds its message `held_uncertain` stages nothing and writes no `queued` row over the `unknown` one (flow/report/relay.go `stage`)). Pinned by `TestFlowDeliveryOfAnUncertainSendIsRecordedUnknownAndItsReadbackAccepted` and, through the daemon, `TestFlowReportE2EAStrandedSendIsRecordedUnknownAndAReplayWritesNoQueuedRowOverIt`; `TestFlowReportE2EDaemonDeathAndResumeNeverWriteTheDeliveryTwice` ("before the settle") now expects the `unknown` row beside the `queued` one.
- A `queued` row the writer appended after the daemon's `unknown` row (the daemon recovered or settled the staged message as `held_uncertain` before the writer appended the row of its staging) superseded it, so `crw flow reports`, the writer's answer and replay and the pump read `queued`: the uncertain send was hidden and the pump did not raise the urgent `jun_decision`; and a replay that read `unknown` while the recipient's readback settled the send appended a `queued` row and answered `queued`, standing (Argon V5 P1 of 118a891c) (flow/report/report.go `SupersedesDelivery`, `Writer.Up`; flow/report/relay.go `stage`); port: fixed (`unknown` is a settled row like `accepted` and `refused`: a `queued` row never supersedes one before it, and only a later settled row does, the readback's `accepted`; every reader reads the rows through `report.SupersedesDelivery`; the writer answers what the rows read once its staging is done, and a replay that finds the message read stages nothing). Pinned by `TestSupersedesDeliveryKeepsAnUnknownRowOverALaterQueuedOne`, `TestAReportReadsItsUnknownDeliveryOverALaterQueuedRow`, `TestAnUncertainSendBeforeTheQueuedRowReadsUnknown`, `TestAReplayWhoseUncertainSendTheReadbackSettlesAnswersAccepted`, `TestPumpRaisesAJunDecisionWhoseUnknownSendAQueuedRowFollowed` and, through the daemon, `TestFlowReportE2EAnUncertainSendBeforeTheQueuedRowReadsUnknown`.

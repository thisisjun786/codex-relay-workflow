# CRW-1334 — a supervisor message to a busy recipient waited out its backoff and a capped one was never released

Source: CRW's own design (CRW-1314 design section 3), not the CXC oracle (CXC v0.2.40, commit 3c1459ac): the oracle's supervisor channel (supervisorchannel.py `_defer_busy`, policy.py `RetryPolicy`) defers a busy recipient on the doubling backoff, holds it at `busy_cap` once its journalled busy count reaches the cap and has no idle edge, and the CRW-904 idle edge was added for deliveries only. The Go side is `internal/relay/supervisor` (`wake.go`, the release in `readback.go`, the busy deferral in `deferral.go` unchanged), `internal/relay/daemon` (`idle_wake.go`, the second hold pass in `daemon.go`) and `internal/relay/store/sendbudget.go` (`OrchestratorClassPrefixes`, `RelationshipIsOrchestrator`).

- A supervisor message whose recipient was busy waited out its whole backoff (15 s doubling to 300 s) even when the recipient turned idle at once, and the daemon held no subscription for its recipient, so no idle report reached it (supervisor/deferral.go, daemon/idle_wake.go `WakeBusyHead` for deliveries only); port: fixed (the daemon keeps the recipient's subscription for an unsent, unheld `deferred_busy` supervisor message, opened in the tick the supervisor pass deferred it, and the recipient's `idle` or `notLoaded` report sets its `next_eligible_at` to the tick, journaled `supervisor_message_woken`; the same tick's supervisor pass attempts it, and a `notLoaded` recipient is loaded by `thread/resume` and given the turn by `turn/start`. Pinned by `TestSupervisorWake_an_idle_edge_makes_a_busy_deferred_message_due`, `TestSupervisorWake_an_idle_edge_does_not_touch_other_states_or_holds`, `TestSupervisorWake_idle_wake_recipients_names_busy_and_capped_recipients`, `TestIdleWake_a_supervisor_message_is_woken_by_its_recipients_edge` and, over the real transport with the scripted App Server, `TestSupervisorWake_end_to_end_a_notLoaded_supervisor_is_resumed_and_given_the_turn`).
- A supervisor message held at `busy_cap` (forty busy answers) or `attempt_cap` was never released by anything (supervisor/deferral.go, supervisor/send.go `settle`; the only releases were `superseded_by_report` and `hierarchy_unresolved` at staging); port: fixed (the recipient's idle or notLoaded edge, or a verified `host_read` readback of another message to the same recipient, lifts the hold and makes the message due, journaled `supervisor_message_released` with the hold and the cause; the busy count is not reset and no other hold is touched, so a released message that is busy past the cap again is held again until the next edge or readback. Pinned by `TestSupervisorWake_an_idle_edge_releases_busy_cap_and_attempt_cap_holds`, `TestSupervisorWake_a_verified_readback_releases_capped_holds_to_that_recipient` and `TestIdleWake_a_capped_supervisor_message_is_released_by_its_recipients_edge`).
- The upward channel's `run:<id>` and `initiative:<key>` relationship ids have no relationships row, so the orchestrator class read from the parent binding never applied and they were held to the ordinary twelve an hour (store/sendbudget.go `RelationshipIsOrchestrator`); port: fixed (they are of class orchestrator by their id, cap 60 an hour; the five-second minimum interval is unchanged. Pinned by `TestRelationshipIsOrchestrator_run_and_initiative_ids_are_of_class_orchestrator` and `TestSendRefusal_run_and_initiative_relationships_take_the_orchestrator_cap`).
- Deviation: a recipient that is not a Codex App Server thread on the relay's host (a Claude Code management session, for example), or one without authorized settings and a readable role policy, is not woken by the relay; its messages are withheld before the send and stay readable with `supervisor-show`; port: kept (the relay's transport is the App Server; documented in docs/relay/supervisor-channel.md).

# CRW-1206 — the milestone close: the final gate, the run-owned QA oracle passes, the reviewer audit

Source: CRW's own design (0.5.0-alpha, issue F13), not the CXC oracle: CXC v0.2.40 (commit 3c1459ac) has the final-gate guard (`final-gate-guard.ts`, ported in `internal/role/spawn/finalgate.go`) and the standalone `crw-qa` skill, and neither is changed. The Go side is `internal/flow/ops/{finalgate,qa,lane_close,audit}.go`, `internal/flow/audit/audit.go`, `internal/flow/cli/{cli,env}.go`, `internal/pabcd/source/commit_identity.go` and, for the mark of the QA call, `internal/flow/engine/{call,backend,digest,journal,policy,fake}.go` and `internal/flow/backend/agent/{agent,thread,fork,bound,worktree}.go`; the script side is `internal/flow/workflows/{project-lane.js,lib/close.js,lib/lane.js,lib/packets.js,lib/schemas.js}`; the rule is written in `docs/flow/script.md`, "The milestone close". The tests are `internal/flow/ops/{finalgate,qa,audit}_test.go`, `internal/flow/audit/audit_test.go`, `internal/flow/cli/cli_test.go`, `internal/flow/scenario/close_test.go`, `internal/flow/backend/agent/qa_test.go`, `internal/flow/engine/qa_test.go`, `internal/pabcd/source/commit_identity_test.go` and `internal/role/spawn/flow_close_test.go`.

- Supersedes CRW-1201.md, item beginning "`crw.milestoneClose` and `crw.reportUp` are the seams of the issues that follow": now port: fixed for the close (the seam `ops.Closer` is gone: the final gate and reviewer-final are `crw.finalGate`, `crw.qaReceipt` and `crw.reviewerFinal` with the composites of `crw:lib/close`, and `crw.milestoneClose(milestone, evidence)` records the tip the close judged and whether the target moved on after the gate), pending (F11) for the delivery of the report, which stays the `Reporter` seam.
- The body says the gate is recorded in `flow_calls` with kind `gate` and the audit with kind `audit`. The kind column of a call row is the op of the call (`journal.md`: "the call's op, `agent` for an agent call"), so the rows are `crw.finalGate` and `crw.audit`; `OpGate` (`ops/proofs.go`, which named `crw.gate`) is the row `crw.finalGate`, and its `command` is the plan's required set joined with `;`. port: kept.
- The body's plan does not say where the required command set, the surfaces of the criteria or the work grade are read from. They are fields of `ops.Milestone` (`required_commands`, `surfaces`, `grade`) that the relay seam fills; a plan that names no command requires `crw-dev ci local`, a milestone with no surface needs no QA, and a grade nobody gave is read as C3 (the two oracle passes are asked for, not skipped). `ops.VerifyRequest` gains `Command`; `CILocalVerifier` verifies `crw-dev ci local` only and refuses another command, which is then a verification that did not run (the milestone is held). port: kept.
- `crw.verify` with no node is the verification of the integrated head for the final gate (`opts`: milestone, command, base), made in a worktree of the repository of the run; the verification record is the same `verification-record/1` the acceptance of a node reads. port: kept.
- Evidence is `stale` when the identity of the head it stands on cannot be resolved or differs (`source.Compare`; an unavailable side is stale, never fresh), as the native hook reads an identity git could not resolve as a receipt that verifies nothing (CRW-1114). `source.CommitIdentity` is the identity of a pinned commit and compares as `Capture` of a clean worktree of it. port: kept.
- The QA capture call is an `executor` call marked `qa: true` (a call option, the prepared row's `qa`, `ThreadSpec.QA`, `ForkSpec.QA`, `TurnSpec.QA`), which the body's edit regions do not name: `internal/flow/engine/` and `internal/flow/backend/agent/` change by the mark only. Without it the call would get the worktree of a node (`call-<id>` at the node's base, not at H), the executor overlay (which lets it start a helper) and the allowance of an executor (1), and its edits would be a pending tree. With it the backend gives it the QA overlay (`roles.Options{QA: true}`, F02), a call-private scratch worktree at the `head` it names, no pending tree and `nativeAllowance` 0. `agent.Config.Prompt` takes `(role, qa)`. port: kept.
- The two oracle passes are run by the run (SP-QA-2) as `explorer` calls with `head` H, in parallel on child paths of one join, and the synthesis is a continuation of the QA call (`resume`), so the pair of each call is the pair of its role and the receipt records it as the thread read it back. If a pass does not complete the synthesis is not asked for and the receipt is INCONCLUSIVE; the gate fails with reason `qa_inconclusive` and the milestone is escalated (the body: "escalation"), not held, even when the pass was only held by the host. port: kept.
- A QA verdict that rests on three verdicts is the worst of them (`PASS` < `REVISE` < `FAIL`): a synthesis cannot lift a pass that failed. A `PASS` over a finding of P0 or P1 is not a valid answer (`DecodeQAPass`, `DecodeQAReceipt`; the rule of a design audit that approves over a P0 or P1). The body does not say either. port: kept.
- reviewer-final (SP-REVF-1) reads the integrated head H through the option `head`, with a packet that carries the gate, and its model family must differ from every author of the milestone (the architect and the executors: `independence_refused`, `independence_unproven`), as a node's reviewer's does; a `null_model` answer is asked once more on a fresh thread. What the lane does with an alignment of `MISALIGNED` or a verdict of `needs_changes` is the issue of the architect's continuity (F09): this issue records the answer and returns the alignment. port: pending (F09).
- The audit reads the packet the reviewer was sent from the host to compare the head and the criteria digest with the acceptance's, so the reviewer packet gained the lines `HEAD: <commit>` and `CRITERIA DIGEST: <digest>` (`packets.js`); a thread whose packet lacks them is a mismatch. The digest of the last `agentMessage` is compared as the engine computes the digest of a result (SHA-256 of the canonical JSON of the one document the message holds). The "observed pair of the executor" is the pair the host read back for the executor threads of the node (the `thread_started` and `fork_started` rows), which is what the independence check of a review rests on; the pairs of native helpers the executor started are `flow_spawn_decisions` rows of F14 and are not read here. port: kept.
- An audit that finds a mismatch escalates the milestone (`audit_mismatch`) and keeps the thread, and the milestone still closes, because the close judged the integrated range and the audit judges the record of the reviews behind it; an acceptance the audit could not observe (unobserved) escalates nothing and keeps its thread. The body does not say what a mismatch stops. port: kept.
- `crw flow audit` appends its result as a row `crw.audit` with a call id of its own (`audit_...`) only when the run has finished (its journal has the run's `finished` row): a run that is still writing owns its journal. The store of a run is opened as `crw flow recover` and `crw flow resume` open it (`store.Open` with the socket of the host), so the command cannot run while the process of the run holds the store. port: kept.
- Files outside the body's edit regions change because of what the regions hold: `internal/flow/script/load_test.go` (the exports of `crw:lib/schemas`), `testdata/flow/shadow/trace.golden.json` (the digest of the helper `crw:lib/schemas`, regenerated with `CRW_UPDATE_GOLDEN=1`; nothing else in it moved), `cmd/crw/main.go` (the route to `crw flow audit`), the scenarios of CRW-1201 (their expected call lists gain `reviewer-final:M1`, and the two close-again scenarios judge the new head again from the gate on), and `docs/flow/script.md` and `docs/flow/journal.md`. `ops.recordOps` gains `qaReceipt`, `reviewerFinal`, `audit` and `archive`. port: kept.
- Correction round after the evaluation of f33bd061 (the body does not say these): a `verified` VERDICT_FINAL is held to the rules of a verified VERDICT and to the criteria of the milestone (`checkFinalVerified`: no criterion not met, no defect of P0 to P2, every criterion c1 to cn once); otherwise it is `null_model` (`verdict_invalid`) and asked once more. The packet of reviewer-final carries the decisions of the approved DESIGN (`GateOutcome.Decisions`, from the ledger row). The QA mark is part of the input digest of a call (only when set, so no other digest changes). A QA call's completed row records the source identity of the directory it ended in (`qa_source`; `engine.SourceReader`, `Backend.SourceOf`), the receipt is INCONCLUSIVE unless it is the clean pinned head, and `crw.qaCapture` resolves the artifact paths of the capture against the worktree it ran in. Deviation: a capture that creates a file outside `.crw/` or edits a tracked file is INCONCLUSIVE even if the edit was needed to drive the surface (the issue says edits are discarded; it does not say the receipt may stand over them). The audit takes the thread of a continued reviewer from its prepared row, reads the packet from the first turn of the call that started the thread, and judges the pair of the executors the recorded commits name (the completed executor calls when no commit is recorded) instead of every prepared executor attempt. `crw flow audit` reports an error of closing the store, the host client or the journal and exits 2. port: kept.

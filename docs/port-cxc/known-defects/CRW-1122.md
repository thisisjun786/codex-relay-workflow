# CRW-1122 — a managed spawn nested too deep is denied before it is issued

Source: CXC v0.2.40 (commit 3c1459ac) `plugins/codexclaw/components/subagent-config/src/spawn-attach-hook.ts:1094-1111` (the
managed issuance, then the `JSON.stringify` of the answer whose `RangeError` the outer catch turns into no output); the Go port's
`spawnHookRoute` and `RunSpawnAttachHook` in `internal/role/spawn/hook_route.go`. Found by the 10-10 CXC improvement review (A5-08).

- The oracle records the managed issuance (`issueManagedSpawn`, :1094-1097) and only then writes the answer, so a claimed
  managed spawn whose `tool_input` carries an unrelated field nested past the writer's depth (7,000 levels in 14,315 bytes) is
  recorded as issued and answered with nothing: the host runs the caller's own input, with the caller's model and effort instead of
  the attempt's candidate. The port answered nothing too, before issuing; port: fixed (a managed spawn whose `tool_input` nests past
  `spawnHookRouteMaxDepth` is denied "managed dispatch: the spawn's tool_input nests deeper than 4463 levels, ..." with nothing
  issued and the ledger untouched; the final-gate refusal still comes first (CRW-858). The answer of a managed spawn is now written
  before the issuance, which is the last step, and a failure after the issuance (a panic recovered by `RunSpawnAttachHook`) is
  answered as an unknown outcome to reconcile, never as the caller's input. Pinned by
  `TestSpawnHookManagedDeepInputIsDeniedBeforeIssuance` (4,463 and 4,464 nested arrays, the first two levels past the bound, and
  7,000; 4,462 is issued once with the candidate) and `TestSpawnHookManagedDeepInputFinalGateStillFirst`).
- Supersedes known-defects.md, section "Found by the CRW-614 spawn hook routing port", item beginning "`JSON.parse` reads any
  depth, but `JSON.stringify` throws": now port: fixed for a managed spawn (above); kept for a direct spawn, which nothing records,
  and whose deep packet still prints nothing as the oracle does (`TestSpawnHookDeepGateItemsWithoutMarkerPrintsNothing`). A
  subagent's grant is checked before either answer (CRW-1118).
- The hook leg ignored the result of its write and always ended with 0, so a managed attempt could be issued and its answer lost while
  the host ran the caller's input (verification round 1); port: fixed (a failed or short write of the answer, the oversized-input deny
  included, ends the leg with status 2, the blocking status of a PreToolUse hook, exported as `spawn.SpawnHookOutputFailed`. What the
  event committed stays: the attempt issued to its tool use id, a spent grant, and the event's record; none is given back. The same
  call delivered again reuses its issuance (the ledger accepts the same non-empty tool use id) and gets the answer of the first
  delivery, a minted or spent grant from the event's record (CRW-1121, CRW-1118). A managed spawn with no grant answers the second
  delivery from the settings and the ledger of that time. Pinned by `TestSpawnLegFailedOutputIsNotSuccessAndTheCallIsAnsweredAgain`
  and `TestSpawnLegFailedOutputKeepsTheEventsGrant`).

# CRW-1216 — the Linear projection of a plan's issues is the plan summary outbox's row per issue; a plan relationship's verdict enqueues no sync_outbox row

Source: the 0.5.0-alpha design issue R08 (CRW-1216, with its R11 stop). This is the port's own addition: CXC v0.2.40 (commit 3c1459ac) enqueues a coordination-document row in `sync_outbox` for every verdict of every relationship (sync.py `enqueue_verdict_in`) and has no plan summary, so there is no oracle line for the rows. The Go files are internal/relay/delivery/sync.go (`VerdictSync`, `ownedByPlanSummary`), internal/relay/dagsched/outbox.go (`summarySubject`, `issueRows`, `IssueRow`, `RowVerdict`, `summaryText`, `EnqueueSummary`, `SummaryStatus`), docs/relay/dag-outbox.md, docs/relay/invariants.md (I-213 and I-247) and docs/relay/agent-execution-readers.md (the rows of outbox.go and delivery/sync.go).

- `sync.py enqueue_verdict_in` writes a `sync_outbox` row for every verdict on a relationship with a coordination-document target (sync.py `enqueue_verdict_in`; port: `delivery/sync.go` `VerdictSync`); port: kept for a relationship that no plan runs, fixed for a relationship a plan runs (the verdict is the issue's row in the plan's summary outbox, so a second Linear projection of it was a duplicate). "A plan runs the relationship" is the relationship's own row in `dag_node_executions` (every release, correction, redefinition and replacement of a plan node leaves one) in a store that holds `dag_summary_outbox`; nothing is journalled for the skipped row. The `sync_outbox` table, its drain (`sync-next`, `sync-claim`, `sync-complete`, `sync-fail`, `sync-retry`) and its readers are untouched; whether they are deleted is decided after the first install that runs agent nodes (F17). Pinned by TestPlanVerdictSkipsSyncOutbox and TestNonPlanVerdictKeepsSyncOutbox.
- The plan summary states one row per live node, for an agent node and for a node a managed child runs: the stage, the newest verdict with its generation and revision, the criteria set, the ruling ordinal, the correction it queued, the child, the findings (one line each, at most twelve in the text, the digest of all of them), the acceptance that stands and the batch that marked it merged. A managed child's verdict is the newest `verdicts` row over the relationships the plan bound to the node; an agent's is the newest completed reviewer call whose result document carries a verdict, its findings the defects of that document. The surface of a node is `executionOf`'s; a node both sides contradict has no surface and no verdict row. port: kept. Pinned by TestSummaryRendersPerIssueRowForAgentAndOversized and TestSummaryRowCarriesVerdictStatus.
- The subject digest of an entry is the digest of the progress and the normalised render of the rows, computed by `summarySubject` for both the replay comparison of `EnqueueSummary` and the `up_to_date` comparison of `SummaryStatus`; `progress.go` is not changed and `dag-summary-status` prints the same fields. A verdict or a finding that changes under the same stage is a new entry that supersedes the open one, and nothing changed is a replay. The definition is a different one from the digest of the progress alone, so the first enqueue after an upgrade is one entry more per plan (the next sequence number, the same content, no duplicate row). port: kept. Pinned by TestSummaryDigestCoversSameStageVerdictChange and TestSummaryStatusUpToDateUsesSubjectDigest.
- The drain authority of the summary outbox is as it was: enqueue, claim, complete, fail and retry are the project's registered parent's, and the task of the store seat is refused with `scope_role_mismatch`. port: kept. Pinned by TestSummaryClaimRefusesStoreSeat.
- The supervisor's obligation discharge (`supervisor/obligation.go` `Reportable`, `supervisor/proposal.go` `ReportableCurrent`, `supervisor/selection.go`) reads a confirmed `sync_outbox` verdict row of the relationship's coordination-document target, as it did. A relationship a plan runs no longer has such a row, so a confirmed summary entry does not discharge the verdict obligation of such a relationship if it has a coordination-document target. Those files are not among the edit regions of CRW-1216. port: pending (the supervisor's discharge learns the summary outbox, or a plan relationship takes no coordination-document target; to be decided with the issue that owns the supervisor channel).

# CRW-1216 — the Linear projection of a plan's issues is the plan summary outbox's row per issue; a plan relationship's verdict enqueues no sync_outbox row

Source: the 0.5.0-alpha design issue R08 (CRW-1216, with its R11 stop). This is the port's own addition: CXC v0.2.40 (commit 3c1459ac) enqueues a coordination-document row in `sync_outbox` for every verdict of every relationship (sync.py `enqueue_verdict_in`) and has no plan summary, so there is no oracle line for the rows. The Go files are internal/relay/delivery/sync.go (`VerdictSync`, `ownedByPlanSummary`), internal/relay/dagsched/outbox.go (`summarySubject`, `issueRows`, `IssueRow`, `RowVerdict`, `summaryText`, `EnqueueSummary`, `SummaryStatus`), docs/relay/dag-outbox.md, docs/relay/invariants.md (I-213 and I-247) and docs/relay/agent-execution-readers.md (the rows of outbox.go and delivery/sync.go).

- `sync.py enqueue_verdict_in` writes a `sync_outbox` row for every verdict on a relationship with a coordination-document target (sync.py `enqueue_verdict_in`; port: `delivery/sync.go` `VerdictSync`); port: kept for a relationship that no plan runs, fixed for a relationship a plan runs (the verdict is the issue's row in the plan's summary outbox, so a second Linear projection of it was a duplicate). "A plan runs the relationship" is the relationship's own row in `dag_node_executions` (every release, correction, redefinition and replacement of a plan node leaves one) on a node `dag_nodes` still holds live, in a store that holds `dag_summary_outbox`; a relationship of a node the plan retired or replaced is not a plan relationship, since no summary row states its verdict, and keeps its `sync_outbox` row (pre-merge evaluation of 3f5342b6, d2). Nothing is journalled for the skipped row. The `sync_outbox` table, its drain (`sync-next`, `sync-claim`, `sync-complete`, `sync-fail`, `sync-retry`) and its readers are untouched; whether they are deleted is decided after the first install that runs agent nodes (F17). Pinned by TestPlanVerdictSkipsSyncOutbox, TestNonPlanVerdictKeepsSyncOutbox and TestRetiredPlanNodeVerdictKeepsSyncOutbox.
- The plan summary states one row per live node, for an agent node and for a node a managed child runs: the stage, the newest verdict with its generation and revision, the criteria set, the ruling ordinal, the correction it queued, the child, the findings (one line each, clipped to 240 characters and at most twelve in the text; the digest covers every finding whole and unclipped, so a finding that differs beyond the clip is another subject, pinned by TestSummaryDigestCoversFindingsBeyondTheDisplayClip), the acceptance that stands and the batch that marked it merged. A managed child's verdict is the newest `verdicts` row over the relationships the plan bound to the node; an agent's is the newest completed reviewer call whose result document carries a verdict, its findings the defects of that document. The surface of a node is `executionOf`'s; a node both sides contradict has no surface and no verdict row. port: kept. Pinned by TestSummaryRendersPerIssueRowForAgentAndOversized and TestSummaryRowCarriesVerdictStatus.
- The subject digest of an entry is the digest of the progress and the normalised render of the rows, computed by `summarySubject` for both the replay comparison of `EnqueueSummary` and the `up_to_date` comparison of `SummaryStatus`; `progress.go` is not changed and `dag-summary-status` prints the same fields. A verdict or a finding that changes under the same stage is a new entry that supersedes the open one, and nothing changed is a replay. The definition is a different one from the digest of the progress alone, so the first enqueue after an upgrade is one entry more per plan (the next sequence number, the same content, no duplicate row). port: kept. Pinned by TestSummaryDigestCoversSameStageVerdictChange and TestSummaryStatusUpToDateUsesSubjectDigest.
- The drain authority of the summary outbox is as it was: enqueue, claim, complete, fail and retry are the project's registered parent's, and the task of the store seat is refused with `scope_role_mismatch`. port: kept. Pinned by TestSummaryClaimRefusesStoreSeat.
- The supervisor's obligation discharge (`supervisor/obligation.go` `Reportable`, `supervisor/proposal.go` `ReportableCurrent`, `supervisor/selection.go` `Selection`) read a confirmed `sync_outbox` verdict row of the relationship's coordination-document target (CXC `supervision.discharge_of`), and a relationship a plan runs no longer has such a row. Left alone, a confirmed summary entry would not have discharged the obligation and an unsent staged report would have stayed deliverable (pre-merge evaluation of 3f5342b6, d1, a P0 regression). Fixed: the three readers also ask `delivery.PlanSummaryCarriesVerdict`, which answers the confirmed entry of the plan's summary outbox in the relationship's coordination-document target enqueued at or after the instant the verdict was decided (an entry reads the newest verdict of every live node in the transaction that enqueues it); an entry that is not confirmed, one enqueued before the verdict, one of another document and one of a retired node does not discharge. The three readers are outside the edit regions of CRW-1216; the change is the minimum that removes the regression (a call next to the `sync_outbox` read, no other change). Pinned by TestPlanSummaryDischargesTheVerdictObligation. port: deviation (the oracle's discharge reads `sync_outbox` only because the oracle enqueues a row for every verdict; the port discharges by the record that holds the verdict). A verdict ruled while its node was live and never carried by a confirmed entry before the plan retired the node stays standing (a retired node has no row to carry it): the supervisor reports it once as it would an unrecorded verdict.

# CRW-682 — the state-copy preflight judges retained records with their own readers

Source: CXC v0.2.40 (commit 3c1459ac) `plugins/codexclaw/components/pabcd-state/src/render-observations.ts:161`
(`nativeObservationLedgerMalformed`, consulted by the Stop hook at `src/hook.ts:1910`) and
`plugins/codexclaw/components/subagent-config/src/fallback-dispatch.ts:432` (`readDispatch`), through the CRW port in
`internal/runtime/install/migrate/classify.go`, `internal/pabcd/hook/renderobs.go`, `internal/role/dispatch_record.go`
and `internal/pabcd/state/restore_record.go`. No recorded corpus answer changes: the CXC corpus does not drive the
state-copy preflight, so no case is tagged intentionally-changed.

- The state-copy preflight judged three families of retained record with its own, looser shape checks rather than with
  the reader that consumes the copy, so it admitted records those readers refuse and carried the damage into the CRW
  state directory: a dispatch record whose attempt was still in `reconcile` (which the ledger reads as unresolved,
  `fallback-dispatch.ts:841`), a dispatch record the ledger's own `readDispatch` refuses (an empty `candidates` array,
  a null attempt id, a missing `spawnIssued`, an attempt candidate unequal to the candidate at its index), and any
  session or single-object JSON record whose bytes are not what its reader can read (a one-byte `{` in
  `sessions/<id>.json` passed the preflight while `ReadStateStrict` called the same file unreadable). This is a
  port-introduced defect of the CRW M2b classifier, not an oracle defect; port: fixed (the three judgements now
  delegate to `role.DispatchRecordStatuses`, `state.RecordUnreadable` and a strict one-object read, and
  `hook.RenderObsLedgerMalformed` for the one JSONL row whose own reader refuses a whole file on a damaged line; the
  red-first cases are `TestClassifyRecords*` in `internal/runtime/install/migrate/classify_records_test.go`).
- The dispatch-record judgement was reported by the CRW-655 pair evaluation as its findings 1 and 2, and the other
  retained records as finding 3 (CRW-655's own record file carries the destination, handle and fallback defects of the
  same classifier); port: fixed (this change is the fix the issue asks for).
- `internal/pabcd/hook/renderobs.go`'s `NativeObservationLedgerMalformed` is the one JSONL reader in the port that
  refuses a whole ledger for a single damaged line; every other JSONL row's reader skips the damaged line, so those
  rows stay unjudged. The oracle consults the same check before it trusts the ledger (`render-observations.ts:161`,
  `hook.ts:1910`), so the preflight refuses `render-observations.jsonl` the same way; port: fixed (the check is reused,
  not reimplemented, through the exported `RenderObsLedgerMalformed` over bytes the caller already holds).
- The preflight still cannot tell a lock a dead process left from a live one, and `bg/*.json` keeps the store's own
  judgement unchanged; neither is touched here; port: kept (unchanged behaviour of this change's scope).

# CRW-1180 — SessionStart output stacked twice by a resume→compact pair, and a dispatch card cell that pins an unusable model

Source: CXC v0.2.40 (commit 3c1459ac) `plugins/codexclaw/components/cxc-ops/src/map-affordance.ts` (`runMapAffordanceSessionStart`), `plugins/codexclaw/components/subagent-config/src/fallback-dispatch-cli.ts:11-35` and `dispatch-card.ts:36-47`, the provider bridge hook and `plugins/codexclaw/components/config-guard/src/cli.ts:167-196`. The Go files are `internal/affordance/hook.go`, `internal/role/fallback_notice.go` and `dispatch_card.go`, `internal/provider/cli.go`, `internal/runtime/install/configguard/selfheal_report.go` and `internal/guidancerecord`. Found by the isolated real-use trial r2 (S4-F4b, S5-R2-F2).

- A resumed session whose first turn compacts runs SessionStart with `source` `resume`, then the compaction, then SessionStart with `source` `compact`. Codex keeps both outputs after the compaction record (about 7 KB in the trial's rollout), so the binding rule, the pointer list, the provider JSON, the flag warning and the dispatch card are in the context twice, because the oracle prints them for every source (`map-affordance.ts`, `fallback-dispatch-cli.ts`, `config-guard/src/cli.ts:167-196`); port: fixed (CRW-1146 already keeps a resume from repeating what the session was given, by the record of `internal/guidancerecord`. A resume that gives the whole text of a leg now also leaves a mark beside that leg's record (`<leg>.resume`, 0600, a small JSON object that holds a generation id, the SHA-256 of the resume's turn id and of the transcript path, the transcript's length when the resume read it and, for a part, a digest, never text), and the first compact start that would say exactly the text the record names, within `guidancerecord.PairWindow` (15 minutes) of the mark, for the same session and leg, and shown by the session's transcript to be the compaction of the resume's own turn (below), takes the mark and says nothing. The next compact, a compact with other text (the role store, `CRW_SPAWN_V1`, the catalog, the command the pointers name, the provider line or the flag state changed between the two), a compact of another session or without a usable session id, and a compact after a start, clear or any other whole output (which removes the mark) say the whole text as before, so a compaction of its own never loses the guidance. The map-affordance leg and the subagent fallback leg use the record they already keep; the provider line and the self-heal flag warning, whose text is live state and which kept no record, say the line on every resume and record it only on a resume (a start writes no record: it only removes a mark that is there), under the legs `provider-bridge` and `self-heal-report`; a record is written only after the hook wrote the whole answer, never after a failed or short write and never by a cancelled hook. The pair is a resume and the compaction of its own turn, and the evidence of the turn is the session's transcript, which every SessionStart payload names (`transcript_path`); the prompt hook is no evidence, because it is a separate, separately trusted hook whose notification can be missing (disabled or untrusted, or an input over 4 MiB that `harness.ReadInput` refuses before the hook can name a session), and the compact payload names no turn. In the isolated trial's codex 0.154.0 rollout (S4 compact1) a resumed session whose first turn compacts records the turn's start, the compaction (a `compacted` record and an item_completed ContextCompaction event naming the turn), then runs the resume's SessionStart hooks and records their answers, then the compact's, and only then records the user's prompt and runs the prompt hook. So a resume leaves a mark only when the end of the transcript (its last 256 KiB) shows that the resume's turn compacted before the resume ran, with nothing of that turn after the compaction but its context, and keeps the turn and where the transcript ended (`guidancerecord.resumeTurn`); the compact takes the pair only when the same transcript has since gained at most 1 MiB of whole records that name no other turn and hold no user prompt, no turn start, end or abort and no compaction, which is what the resume's own answers are (`compactOfTurn`). A compact of a later turn finds the earlier turn's prompt and end in between and says the whole text, whether or not that turn's prompt reached the prompt hook; so does a compaction later in the resume's turn, after its prompt, and a resume between turns or before its turn compacted leaves no mark. No transcript, an unreadable or different one, one that shrank, a record that does not parse or is still being written, or a longer gain is no evidence, and the compact says the text: a duplicate costs tokens, a lost text costs the guidance. The cxc-ops user-prompt-submit leg (`guidancerecord.NoteUserPrompt`, by session and `turn_id`) still counts prompts against every mark of the session, and a prompt of a second turn, or a second one when the payload names no turn, removes the mark early; it never lets a compact take a pair. Replaying the trial rollout through this rule pairs each of its three resume→compact turns and refuses the compact of the following turn (evidence log `trial-rollout-replay.log` of the lane); it was not exercised against a live Codex. A resume that gave only the session binding and the PATH banner (the session was given the pointers before) leaves a mark of that part (its SHA-256, never the text), and the compact of its turn says the pointers and leaves exactly that binding and banner out, because the context holds them after the compaction record; a banner or binding that differs is said. Every start of a leg, whether or not it has anything to say (the self-heal flag on at a clear or startup, a resume that gave nothing), ends a pair a resume left open, so a later compact is not matched to a stale resume. The hooks are separate processes, so a mark is written once, whole, under a generation of its own, and afterwards only deleted: a prompt counts by creating a file beside the mark (named by the generation and the turn, so a repeat of a turn counts once), never by rewriting the mark, and a compact takes the pair by deleting the mark. A resume writing its record with its mark, and a compact taking the pair, hold an exclusive `flock` on the session's record directory (no lock file is left); a hook that cannot take the lock within two seconds ends the pair by deleting the mark and writes nothing, and nothing, not even a prompt hook that was held up while it held the lock, can bring a deleted mark back, so a resume pairs with at most one compact and an ended pair stays ended.

# CRW-1592 — session marker reuse and load-sensitive test premises

Source: concurrent flow-engine and plan-cycle verification. These are port-owned behavior and fixtures.

- A newly prepared generation reused its call's first session marker. If marker discovery beat the new dispatch report, it could bind the interrupted thread of the previous generation and hold with `host_interrupted_external`. Session attempts now increase across prepared generations. The prepared row reserves that attempt before publication, and recovery and held resumption preserve the reservation. Existing journals without the field keep their original first-attempt default. The evidence lifetime cases force marker discovery before the new spawn, and another regression crashes between preparation and publication; port: fixed (product).
- `AssignmentPublished` inspected an unclaimed record after making the fake child complete. Marker discovery could harvest the child and remove the record before the inspection callback. The fixture now keeps the child running until the callback has inspected the record, then completes it; port: fixed (test isolation).
- The plan-cycle fixture used to restore P1 at the unit check, concurrently with the signal reading P2. CRW-1584 already moved that restoration to P2's design audit and added an ordering regression. CRW-1592 retains that fix and verifies it under the same concurrent workload; no new plan-cycle product change is required.

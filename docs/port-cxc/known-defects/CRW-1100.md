  keeps the later ones open, where a late sign-off can still approve a plan the session no longer runs
  (`orchestrate-cli.ts`:1088-1096, `review-round.ts`:309); port: fixed (every open plan_audit round this

- The cleanup runs before the state is published and its error is dropped (the oracle's catch is fail-open),
  so a state write that fails leaves the rounds closed and their rows written beside a session still at P, and
  a cleanup that fails leaves the session at A with its old rounds open and nothing saying so (`:1097-1119`);
  port: fixed (the rounds to close are listed under the plan's write lock after the binding is revalidated
  and ride the transition's outbox event with the new epoch, so the binding and the cleanup are one recorded
  piece of work; the state is published first and the rounds are closed after it, in the same lock. A write
  that fails before the rename drops the event and leaves the plan as it was. A cleanup that fails after the
  publication stays pending with the event: the answer carries "the re-plan's cleanup of this session's
  earlier plan_audit rounds is pending: ...", and the next orchestrate call or hook of the session replays the

  `TestOrchestrateReplanFailedStateWriteChangesNothing`, `TestOrchestrateReplanCleanupFailureIsFinishedByTheNextCall`).

The cleanup is not a gate: the A transition is never refused or undone for it, and A>B without a verdict stays
advisory (LEAN-REVIEW-01). A late sign-off on a closed round is refused by the round state, and the session
keeps the binding the re-plan made (`TestOrchestrateReplanLateSignoffKeepsTheBinding`). The chat P>A mints a
binding but has no housekeeping in the oracle either, and is unchanged.

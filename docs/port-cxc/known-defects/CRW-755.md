# CRW-755 — orchestrate A>B review binding and B>C source gate

Source: CXC v0.2.40 (`3c1459ac`) `plugins/codexclaw/components/pabcd-state/src/orchestrate-cli.ts`
`validateReviewBinding` :49-95 and its A>B call :688-691, and the B>C source gate :701-717, ported to Go as
`internal/pabcd/cli/orchestrate_review_binding.go` with the `orchestrate-cli.test.ts` and
`review-binding.test.ts` cases of that range as `internal/pabcd/cli/orchestrate_review_binding_test.go`.
The recorded corpus has no fixture this issue can claim on its own: the orchestrate fixtures need the CLI
verb row, which a sibling issue owns, so they stay pending and nothing here is tagged identical or
intentionally-changed. The two A>B cases are driven through the real transition instead.

- A round whose binding is incomplete - a missing `ownerSessionId`, `workPhaseId` or `planEpoch`, or a
  `planFiles` list `reviveReviewRounds` dropped because one entry was malformed or left the working
  directory - is a "pre-binding" round that the A>B check skips (`return null`), so its recorded verdict, a
  reviewer FAIL included, is not honoured, although the field comment says the gate reads a missing binding
  as a refusal and that nothing is grandfathered into approval (source
  `plugins/codexclaw/components/pabcd-state/src/goalplan.ts:200-205` against `orchestrate-cli.ts:76-79`;
  cases `binding_empty`, `binding_mixed` and `files_one_bad_entry_null` in
  `internal/pabcd/goalplan/testdata/oracle-revive.json` show the fields dropped); port: fixed (the
  security finding this issue was split off to close: the gate refuses such a round on A>B instead of
  waving it through, with the text the issue body fixes, and `TestOrchestrateReviewBindingSecurityFix` and
  `TestOrchestrateReviewBindingRecordedBadEntryNull` pin it; the oracle's passing half - a round with NO
  recorded verdict still advances - is kept, in `TestOrchestrateReviewBindingOpenRoundPasses`).
- The two arms of the B>C gate keyed on the absence of `phaseEntrySource` and on a source ROOT that moved
  cannot both be reached from a legal edge, because every legal B entry records the snapshot and only
  `crw relay session source` can move the root, and that command refuses a session past A (source
  `orchestrate-cli.ts:701-708`); port: kept (both arms are ported as they are, in
  `orchestrateReviewBindingSourceGate`; the tests reach them by seeding the state directly).
- The B>C gate compares the B entry snapshot against a capture taken with `excludeCodexclawArtifacts`
  hard-coded true, so a session whose source work happens in its own cwd - where the B entry capture took
  the session's own option - compares a snapshot that excluded `.crw/` against one that does not, and a
  write under `.crw/` alone reads as "the source changed" (source `orchestrate-cli.ts:705` against
  `session-source-identity.ts:5-8`); port: kept.
- Supersedes `known-defects.md`, section "Found by the goalplan record types and sub-record revival port",
  item beginning "A round whose binding is missing": now port: fixed for the A>B gate (the reader still
  drops the incomplete binding, which is what the revival item records; what changed is that the transition
  no longer reads a dropped binding as permission to advance).

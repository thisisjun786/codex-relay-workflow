CRW-904: release a busy delivery head when its recipient reports idle

## The problem

A delivery that finds its recipient mid-turn waits a doubling backoff (15 s to 300 s, capped at 40 answers), and that timer was the only thing that ended the wait. The head of a parent's receipt line therefore held every younger delivery behind it for up to five minutes at a time: the 2026-10-06 measurement had six completion events to one parent waiting up to 80 minutes. Section 82 of the CRW-781 decision chose the recipient's own idle edge as the release, and this is its slice 1.

## What it does

While a head delivery waits out its busy backoff, the relay keeps its own subscription on the recipient, and the App Server's `thread/status/changed` reporting `idle` or `notLoaded` releases the head:

* **The wake is a durable record, not a moved deadline.** One appended store-zone table, `delivery_wakes(event_id, woken_at, original_deadline, spent_at)`, holds one row per woken delivery. `WakeBusyHead` writes it in a single `INSERT OR IGNORE ... SELECT` from the same derived table that names the head, so nothing can be woken twice and nothing between the read and the write can take the place. The row makes the head due, keeps it the head of the line and admits it to `Attempt` and the claim; every reader judges it only against a row that is still the `deferred_busy` one the wake was written for.
* **The woken head keeps the line until the wake is spent.** `busyHeadSQL` names the oldest delivery waiting out a busy backoff whose wake has no `spent_at`, whatever its `next_eligible_at` says, so a woken head that was not claimed before its old deadline cannot be overtaken by a younger delivery of the same recipient. The claim spends the wake as the attempt begins, so the line is held exactly as long as the wake is unspent and never past the claim.
* **`due = min(original, recomputed)`.** The wake records the deadline the delivery carried into the attempt and the claim leaves that record in place, so a busy answer to that attempt — including one reconciled after an uncertain outcome, and one that arrives after the original deadline has passed — never pushes the safety-net timer later.
* **The subscription is the relay's own.** `Client.HoldThread` sends one override-free `thread/resume` (only `threadId` and `excludeTurns`) and records its own hold, taken even when a watch or the bridge's retention already subscribed the thread; `ReleaseThread` drops only that hold. A refused resume is not retried before the delivery's own busy deadline, so the existing backoff curve stays the pacing.
* **An unspent wake does not outlive its wait.** A delivery that leaves `deferred_busy` by a withhold or a supersession without ever being claimed has its wake ended with that transition, so a later ordinary busy deferral cannot inherit an expired deadline.
* **The pass is bounded and fails closed.** The notification channel stays at its existing 64 and drops to the backoff, and the two store failures this pass can meet are handed to the daemon's own halt rather than recorded as notes: the wake is the pass's own write, the read of the waiting heads is an observation, so a failure of CRW-848's halting class ends the tick before the delivery pass and the supervisor channel would write into a store the relay has just seen damaged.

No refusal reason, output field or CLI is added. I-478 in `docs/relay/invariants.md` names the marker, the spent rule and the subscription.
